What is CourtVision?

CourtVision is an AI-native NBA prop-betting intelligence platform. It scrapes live odds from DraftKings, FanDuel, Pinnacle, BetRivers, and 10+ other books, then overlays player-performance predictions from a trained machine-learning model stack to surface positive-EV wagers. The platform updates in real time via WebSocket line feeds and flags sharp money (steam) moves as they happen.

How the model works

Seven player-prop statistics (PTS, REB, AST, FG3M, STL, BLK, TOV) are each predicted by an ensemble of XGBoost, LightGBM, and neural-network models trained on 85 engineered features — rolling form (L5/L10/EWMA), opponent defense, pace, rest, and CV-derived behavioral signals. Primary predictions use quantile (q50) regression so the model targets the median outcome — the same scoring target the sportsbook's O/U line is set against. Walk-forward cross-validation over two NBA seasons gates every model change before it ships.

Key terms
Edge (pp)
model_p_over − market_implied_p_over. Positive = model sees more probability of hitting the OVER than the market price implies. 1 pp = 1 percentage point.
EV %
Expected value per $100 staked = (model_p × decimal_payout − 1) × 100. Positive EV means the model expects a profit at the posted price.
Kelly %
Quarter-Kelly stake as % of bankroll = (edge / decimal_odds) × 0.25. Capped at 5% per leg to limit variance.
Steam (🌊)
A sudden, coordinated line movement across multiple books in a short window — a signal that sharp money (professional bettors) is hitting this market. Steam does not by itself constitute a recommendation.
Best O / Best U
The highest available OVER or UNDER price across all scraped books for that prop at the moment of last capture. Line-shop here before placing a bet.
CLV
Closing Line Value — how the price moved between when CourtVision recommended it and when the market closed. Positive CLV means the model was ahead of the closing line, a strong long-run predictor of profitability.
Using the parlay builder
  1. 1 Go to /odds and click any prop row to open its detail panel.
  2. 2 Click + OVER or + UNDER to add the leg to the parlay builder (sidebar, right side).
  3. 3 Add 2–5 legs. Combined American odds and $100 payout update live.
  4. 4 Click Save Parlay to record it, or visit /parlays for auto-generated EV-ranked combinations.
Risk panel & kill switch

The Risk Control card on the right side of /odds displays your bankroll, today's P&L, 30-day drawdown, and open bet count. A daily loss cap (default 5% of bankroll) is enforced.

The Kill Switch immediately disables all bet recommendations and deeplinks. Use it during system outages, unexpected line moves, or any time you want a clean stop. Disengage when ready to resume.

Feedback & bug reports

This is a private research tool. To file bugs or suggest improvements, open a GitHub issue or drop a note in the project vault at vault/Sessions/Decision Log.md. For data issues (missing player, wrong line), check data/lines/ and verify the scraper ran for today's date.
