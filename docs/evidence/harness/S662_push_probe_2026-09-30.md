# S662 -- MLB push-doorbell vs 10 s REST poll, paired receipt lag (2026-09-30)

VERDICT: MEASURED; INSUFFICIENT for the register acceptance (2 games paired, the rule needs >= 10). The S593 build
gate (push arm p50 <= 11 s on >= 10 games) is NOT DECIDABLE from tonight. On the 222 paired pitch events tonight,
the push arm's p50 is 11.09 s, just above the 11 s gate (effectively one game: 199 of 222 pairs are game 849841). The poll arm's p50 is 22.57 s. The paired poll-minus-push p50 is
10.52 s.

Paper only. This is an execution-quality (data freshness) measurement, not a model result.

## What was measured

- **Poll arm (i).** This is the running 10 s state capture, read only and tail-only from the probe's start offset.
  An event's poll receipt is the first capture row whose `state.last_play_id` is that event.
- **Push arm (iii).** This is our own socket at `wss://ws.statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/game/push/subscribe/gameday/{gamePk}`.
  - It sends `Origin: https://www.mlb.com`. Without that header the handshake returns HTTP 500.
  - It makes exactly one diffPatch GET per updateId, with `startTimecode` set to the held feed's timecode.
  - When a patch fails, it refetches the full feed.
- **Stamp.** The event's `startTime` in the final applied feed. No startTime was revised (n_start_revised = 0).
- **Headline events.** Only events first surfaced by an incremental diff count. The paired difference is receipt
  minus receipt, so the stamp and the laptop's clock offset both cancel out of it.

### Selection and coverage (game 849841)

- 297 events carry a stamp.
  - 251 were first seen by the push arm.
  - 238 were seen by the poll arm, and every one of them was also seen by push.
  - 238 are paired, and 199 of those are headline.
- The 39 non-headline pairs:
  - 28 diff_full, where the server returned the whole feed.
  - 7 full refetch after a patch failure.
  - 4 first-after-reconnect.
- Poll censoring: 59 of 297 stamped events were never the capture's `last_play_id` at a poll (superseded
  within one 10 s poll, or hidden because the capture's `last_play_id` falls back to an integer at-bat index;
  286 of 949 capture rows carry an integer). Paired poll lag is CONDITIONAL on the event surviving to a poll;
  the direction of that bias is not verified.
- Game 849846 run 2 paired kinds: diff 23, diff_full 3, full 1, first-after-reconnect 3.

### Where the lag lives

Pooled p50, measured on the push arm:

- **Server publish, about 7.7 s.** This is the doorbell message's own `timeStamp` minus the pitch's startTime,
  at 1 s resolution.
- **Receipt minus message timeStamp, about 7.0 s** (p10 2.0 s, p90 7.7 s). This is NOT network transport: it
  includes any hold between the stamp and the send, and the laptop-vs-MLB clock offset. Component medians are
  not additive (7.7 + 7.0 is not the 11.0 total; the two correlate at r = -0.27; timeStamp has 1 s resolution).
- **diffPatch fetch, under 0.1 s.**

Every doorbell carried `"isDelay": true, "wait": 10` (506 of 506 on game 849841). The p50 of about 11 s sits
close to that 10 s figure, which suggests the server holds updates for a delay window. That link is not
verified.

Inferred, not measured: the poll arm adds its 0-10 s poll phase and the CDN `max-age=10` on top of the same
publish point.

## Socket payload kind (resolves the S593 vs memo F disagreement)

The socket message is more than a bare id. Each one carries:

- `timeStamp`, `gamePk`, `updateId`, `wait`, `isDelay`
- `logicalEvents` (e.g. `countChange`, `count12`)
- `gameEvents` (e.g. `foul`, `ball`, `game_advisory`)
- `changeEvent`

It carries NO playId, count values or state. It is a typed doorbell: the event kind comes on the socket, and the
state needs the diffPatch.

## Failure modes seen (inputs for any S593 build)

1. **Handshake HTTP 500s.** Game 849841 had 2 before its first open. Run 2 had 3, then opened at 21:22:06Z.
   Retries were spaced 30 s apart.
2. **A subscription opened pre-game went silent.** Game 849846's socket in run 1 was opened at 18:41:33Z and stayed
   connected. Its last doorbell arrived at 20:46:10Z. The capture recorded 15 new play ids from 21:11:59Z up to
   the null (16 including one at 21:20:41Z, after the probe ended), and the socket delivered none of them. The
   probe declared NULL_NO_DOORBELL at 21:19:54Z: 15 min after the feed showed Live status (warmup included),
   about 8 min after the first pitch.
   - A fresh subscription (run 2) got its first doorbell about 6.5 s after opening (21:22:06.3Z to 21:22:12.8Z;
     n = 1 subscription).
   - This is CONSISTENT WITH a stale subscription, but a service-side gap is not excluded: the old and new
     sockets never overlapped, and run 2's handshake got HTTP 500 three times at 21:20:35-21:21:39Z. The next
     run should open a second socket concurrently when silence is detected.
   - S593 needs a watchdog that re-subscribes when REST shows new plays and the socket is silent.
3. **diffPatch ops that our strict RFC 6902 apply rejected.** 45 of 506 updates failed on game 849841. 41 of those
   were `list assignment index out of range`. Each one cost a full refetch. 91 diffs were empty (`[]`),
   consistent with fetching before the `wait` window elapsed.
4. **endTime is not a pitch time.** Against the final endTime, push lags go negative (p50 -5.5 s), because endTime
   is revised later. It is reported only as a secondary stamp.

## Limits

- Total probe wall time was 2 h 38 min plus 21 min, under the 3 h limit.
- Output went to a scratch root, and the archive was never written.
- The capture, the Windows task and the schedules were not touched.
