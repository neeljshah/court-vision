## 5. m1_api_paper selfcheck, before and after

BEFORE (pid 19239): `curl localhost:8099/ready` ->
`{"status":"not_ready","selfcheck":{"checked":["/api/paper/predictions","/health","/api/sports"],"present":["/health","/api/sports"],"missing":["/api/paper/predictions"],"ok":false}}`

AFTER (pid 24212): byte-identical body -- still `not_ready`, still
`missing:["/api/paper/predictions"]`.

The route itself is FIXED and serving. Measured on pid 24212:

- `curl "localhost:8099/api/paper/predictions?limit=1"` -> **HTTP 200**, body
  `{"status":"ok","total":0,"offset":0,"limit":1,"trades":[],"edge_claimed":false,"real_money_enabled":false,...}`
- `/openapi.json` lists `/api/paper/predictions` among 12 `/api/paper/*` paths.
- The `quant_routes mount failed (... build_validation ... unknown location)` line
  appears in `logs/m1_api_paper.err` only for the 19239 boot, not for 24212.

So `/ready` is a FALSE NEGATIVE, and the cause is in `supervisor/health.py`, not in
anything deployed here. A fresh in-process import of `predict_service.app` yields 46
routes and **zero** `/api/paper/*` paths, while the served app has 12 -- the paper
routers are registered after module execution, but `mount_selfcheck` (health.py:213)
runs at module scope (app.py:579) and **caches its result on the app object**
(health.py:222-224), so `/ready` keeps returning that pre-registration snapshot for
the life of the process. Filed as a new gap; `supervisor/**` is not this lane's to edit.
