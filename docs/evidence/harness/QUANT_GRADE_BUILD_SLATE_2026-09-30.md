7. NBA state access: cdn.nba.com returns 403 from the laptop and the mechanism (headers or IP range) is untested (memo F sections 1c and 5).
8. Execution risk: no exposure netted to a game outcome, no per-sport / per-hour / slate caps, no breakers for feed lag, latency, markout deterioration or drawdown in contracts, no risk receipts (memo E section 2, holes 1-6); S555 ladder DEFERRED.
9. Evidence chain: raw shards have no immutable availability record; S550's shard identity misses a same-size mid-shard edit (GAP_REVIEW 1.3); preregs embed no corpus manifest (memo G R6).
10. Multiplicity: every per-candidate statistic in the multiplicity memo is NA because loss differentials were never archived (memo G R2).
11. Governance: no model card, no model inventory, reviewer independence unstated, no postmortem, no numeric service level (memo G G1, G2, R5, P4, P3); memo G tally HAVE 14, PARTIAL 11, MISSING 3 of 28.
12. Power and subset honesty: no desk-level power ledger (GAP_REVIEW 1.6); "fresh venue mid" has no sealed maximum book age (GAP_REVIEW 1.7); capture coverage is 12-32 pct of served-game time (DESK_STATE section 4).
13. Models: the MLB in-game model trails the contemporaneous Kalshi price over 227 games (JOB_EVIDENCE_PACKET.md:96 via GAP_REVIEW section 4); shops anchor on the market and price deviations (memo B section 8), and a consensus-of-books baseline is absent (memo B section 6).

15. What the desk already has that shops often lack: hash-sealed preregs, a trial ledger read at launch, immovable bars, second-corpus rule, supersede-by-adding evidence (memo G section 0). The rows below add the operational layer around it.
