## AMENDMENT 1 fix 1b: post-exit peak read

After the poll loop the runner reads the OS peak once more
(`paper_night_peak.finish`): on Windows PeakWorkingSetSize via ctypes
GetProcessMemoryInfo on the child handle Popen still holds after wait; elsewhere
os.wait4 ru_maxrss (the poll loop uses waitid WNOWAIT so the child stays
unreaped until wait4). peak_working_set_bytes = max(polled, post-exit);
peak_method is `windows_peak_wset_polled+post_exit`, or
`windows_peak_wset_polled_lower_bound` when the post-exit read is unavailable.
A post-exit peak above the ceiling marks the step reason
ceiling_exceeded_post_exit (added to the closed reason set) and the night
PARTIAL, though the child finished. Probe (child sleeps 0.2 s, allocates
600 MB, exits; ceiling 200 MB; poll 0.5 s): polled 2,056,192 bytes, post-exit
615,579,648 bytes, so the recorded peak is 615,579,648 (>= 600 MB). The test
`test_post_exit_peak_catches_spike_between_polls` repeats this with a 50 MiB
spike, a 32 MiB ceiling and a 0.5 s poll: step RAN exit 0, reason
ceiling_exceeded_post_exit, night PARTIAL.

## RAM-guard arithmetic

- Trigger: ram_guard fires at vm.percent > 94, i.e. below 973,974,405 of
  16,232,906,752 bytes free.
- Floor: ceiling 1,572,864,000 + margin 1,048,576,000 = 2,621,440,000 bytes
  available required before a child starts.
- A child starting at the floor and reaching its ceiling leaves
  2,621,440,000 - 1,572,864,000 = 1,048,576,000 bytes free, above the
  973,974,405 trigger. Outside growth after admission can still fire the guard.
