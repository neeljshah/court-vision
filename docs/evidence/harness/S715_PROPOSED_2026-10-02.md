Attempt one queued date per tick through `python -m scripts.platformkit.ops.schedule_recovery --date DATE --queue PATH --books-root BOOKS --state-root STATE --out-dir OUT --sports SPORTS --local-date-tz TZ`. Journal its JSON refusal or completion. A refused date remains queued. If recovery succeeds, continue to the ordinary D1 branch. A date before the current UTC date stays unresolved for owner review, because its starts are no longer prospective.
