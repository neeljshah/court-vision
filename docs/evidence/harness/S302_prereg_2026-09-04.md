# S302 Preregistration

## Scope

This preregistration is sealed before the S302 construct is implemented or
scored. It covers one local fixture with one target state and one future,
same-team planted state 47 hours later.

## Fixed design

- Route: `scripts/platformkit/eval_gate/cpcv_engine.py:cpcv_evaluate`.
- CPCV design: four frozen groups, one test group, and `embargo_days=1`.
- Target: `g0`, at `2024-07-01T19:00:00`.
- Plant: `g2`, at `2024-07-03T18:00:00`, sharing team `AAA` with `g0`.
- Predictor: return 0.9 when the plant is in its train set; otherwise return
  0.5.
- Real condition: use the unmodified evaluator. The plant must be absent from
  the target train set and the target probability must equal 0.5 exactly.
- Control: fixture-level monkeypatch of only `_blocked_indices` to leave the
  CPCV train indices unblocked. The target score must differ from the real
  condition.

## Metric and interpretation

The recorded metrics are plant absence, target probability under real purge,
target probability under the disabled-purge control, and their difference.
The difference convention is `control probability - real-purge probability`;
nonzero means disabling the purge exposed the plant. This is a construct test,
not a calibration comparison or a claim about a production model.

## Acceptance bar

`absent=True`; real-purge probability exactly `0.5`; control difference not
equal to `0`. Enumeration is exhaustive: one planted case and one control.
