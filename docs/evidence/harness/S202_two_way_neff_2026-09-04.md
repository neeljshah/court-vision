## Method and archive

`scripts/platformkit/eval_gate/s202_two_way_neff.py` uses 1,000 crossed
pigeonhole-bootstrap resamples with seed 20260904. For each resample it draws
both label dimensions independently with replacement and gives each original
row the product of its two sampled-label multiplicities. It computes
mean-equivalent n_eff as sample_variance divided by bootstrap_mean_variance.
All original rows remain in the fixed denominator; no cluster-size filter is
used and no small cluster is dropped.

The four per-row archives are under
`docs/evidence/S202_two_way_neff_2026-09-04/`. Each carries event_id,
event_date, y, p_base, the reconstructible S05 walk-forward recalibrated
probability, both Brier losses, their paired differential, and all label keys.
The summary JSON at `docs/evidence/S202_two_way_neff_2026-09-04.json` records
input full paths, byte sizes, tabular-resolution marker, SHA-256 hashes, route
SHA-256 hashes, archive SHA-256 hashes, cluster counts, seed, and every metric.
The recalibrated arm names the existing S05 preregistration and its seal
`9051BB6E3BD89F7309A799F9739C8E61EA6DB3530E52AD87666568220591DF8A`.

## Q9 reproduction

The verifier-style reproduction opened only the emitted CSV archive and the
summary JSON, then reran both estimators at the recorded seed. The maximum
absolute one-way difference was 7.83e-11 and the maximum absolute two-way
difference was 6.37e-12, attributable to CSV floating-point round trips. The
per-row archive alone contains both loss series, timestamps, and cluster labels
needed for the recomputation.

## NOT VERIFIED

- This diagnostic measures sampling dependence only. It makes no claim about a
  change in calibration quality.
- The S05 recalibrated arm is reconstructed from its named sealed method and is
  also stored per row here; no independent external recalibration was run.
- The crossed-bootstrap n_eff is a bootstrap mean-equivalence estimate, not a
  general replacement for every possible clustered variance estimator.
