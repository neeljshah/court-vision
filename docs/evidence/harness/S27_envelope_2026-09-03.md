## Staleness policy asserted (exactly the plan's, nothing added)

- Bound declared per probe as `max_ok_staleness_days`: injuries 7 d (I01-I03),
  analytics receipts 48 h (R01-R05), execution_status 48 h (X06). An `ok` older
  than the bound is a violation, and an `as_of` the test cannot parse is also a
  violation -- a bound that cannot be evaluated does not bind.
- Pinned corpora (win_probability, profiles-backed scouting/comparables) are NOT
  refused on age; they must instead report `staleness_days`, and their `as_of`
  must describe the DATA: an `as_of` within 900 s of the call is the wall clock
  (`OK_ASOF_IS_WALL_CLOCK`), which is exactly how P2 was measured here.
- Existence test for "the artifact is a file": `source_artifact` (string or list)
  resolved against the repo root, backslashes normalised.
