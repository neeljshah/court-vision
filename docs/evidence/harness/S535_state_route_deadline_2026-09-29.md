## MANDATORY DISCLOSURES

- (i) no real 403/429 refusal was ever observed (0/0 in the heartbeats above);
- (ii) per-tick wall time is unmeasured live; the heartbeat is throttled to about 60 s;
- (iii) ROUTE_DEADLINE_SEC = 7.0 is NOT PROFILED under live refusals; retune via the constructor;
- (iv) a request already in flight is not interrupted; requests applies its 15 s timeout per connect/read operation, so the overshoot is normally <= 15 s but not a hard bound;
- (v) a skipped or held league is missing data for that tick (soccer also counts soccer_league_errors for it, a truthful double signal); an unclamped shared pacer was the pre-fix hazard;
- (vi) Retry-After "86401" is treated as absent (parse error, wait 1/rate): landed behaviour, pinned;
- (vii) adoption needs docs/operations/relaunch_state2_s535_2026-09-29.ps1 run between games by the orchestrator; this row did NOT run it;
- (viii) stuck routes cost again every time their holds expire together (about every 65 s), not only once: adversarial r2 sim.py, 3 stuck soccer routes with no Retry-After -> MLB interval p90 21.11 s, max 22.11 s (1 stuck: p90 10.00 s); 6 stuck: max 38.31 s; all 11 ESPN routes answering 429 with no Retry-After -> MLB gap up to 76.11 s and holds never engage because the 300 s soccer poll cadence exceeds the 60 s hold (81.80 s without the B1 restore, so not caused by B1); a per-tick budget is a follow-up only if observed live;
- (viii-b) B1 by design: after a skip each new route starts back at the restored rate, so a persistent host-wide 429 storm is not reflected in the pacer rate (adversarial r2 hw.py: 96 ESPN requests in 10 min, min gap 0.565 s, 0% of ticks at the floor, vs 74 / 1.200 s / 29% without the restore); each route is still capped at 4 requests per poll by the deadline;
- (viii-c) the hold key has no query string, so a refused call for yesterday's MLB schedule also holds today's schedule call for at least 60 s (pinned by the B2 tick test); known games keep polling their live feeds;
- (ix) B3: an ESPN 429 may be host-wide, but the hold is per route: a sibling ESPN route goes out about 2.2 s after a skipped 429 with Retry-After 30 (adversarial r1 p34.py; A3 by design);
- (x) B3: a large Retry-After on the FINAL refusal inside the deadline (left on the shared pacer by landed S341 on_429) is unreachable at the production max_retries=5 (the minimum waits 0.667 + 1.333 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8 s exceed 7 s, so the pre-check aborts first) and reachable only at max_retries <= 3;
- (xi) B3: recovery resumes at the first poll after the hold expires, not the next tick: 78.2 s for Retry-After 60, 77.7 s for an absent header (re-probes at 8.2, 9.4, 11.6, 13.8 s), 318.0 s for 300 (adversarial r2 rec.py on the B1 candidate, soccer cadence).
