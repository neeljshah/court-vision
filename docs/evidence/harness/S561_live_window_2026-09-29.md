## Mandatory disclosures

- Rule: each constant is the largest offset measured on current listings; too large flags live
  early (safe: live only reorders focus and adds the ticker to the trades rotation), too small late.
- LIMIT: an event Kalshi edits to +6 h (1 of 32 nfl, KXNFLGAME-26SEP28PHICHI) flags live at start + 3 h.
- The nba +6 h appeared in the 09-25 Kalshi update and may revert; re-measure on the first 10-20
  slate. If nba returns to +3 h the constant is 10800.
- LANDING IS ADOPTION: the label definition changes at the relaunch instant; no live-cadence or
  census comparison may span it without splitting there. Paper desk / arm E read the STATE
  capture status, not the books label, so no paper or arm E decision moves.
- Per-path malformed-timestamp behaviour differs (int 123: trades/running live, legacy close_time;
  naive: trades close_time, running idle + error, legacy UTC); pinned, not unified (out of scope).
- S556 overlap: only the mlb 30 min early flag; the first-pitch-delay residual stays S556.
- Calibration / capture-coverage language only; no archive, registry or flag touched.
