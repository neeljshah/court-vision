## Finding

CONFIRMED. `docs/evidence/HARNESS_GAPS_2026-09-03.md:817` requires a 60 MB
log to rotate to five files. On reachable input, `rotation_plan()` in
`scripts/platformkit/ops/disk_guard_paths.py:75-99` returned metadata only.

Fix: `execute_rotation()` applies a `writer_stopped` plan in its declared,
highest-generation-first order using `ops.atomic_replace.replace_with_retry`.
It is not called by the guard, so protected production paths remain selected
out and rotation remains an explicit owner action.

Construct test: `test_60mb_log_rotates_to_five_files` creates a 60 MB live log
and generations .1 through .5, executes the plan, and asserts exactly .1
through .5 remain; .1 has the former live size and .5 has the former .4 bytes.
The unfixed module failed the test at the call site with:

`AttributeError: module 'scripts.platformkit.ops.disk_guard_paths' has no attribute 'execute_rotation'`

The restored fix passed that test: one passed.
