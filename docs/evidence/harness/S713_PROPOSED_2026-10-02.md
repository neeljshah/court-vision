PAPER ONLY. This unified diff is for S717 review. It applies to the current
`scripts/platformkit/ops/paper_desk_tick.py` but is not installed by S713.
S717 must supply `Desk.unresolved_night_sources()` returning three dictionaries:
receipt states, journaled resolution rows, and schedule states. Missing reader
wiring is an explicit refusal. Queue access remains inside the existing tick lock.

On the next scheduled tick after S717 adoption, this hook reads active night
attention and expiry evidence, persists the queue, and scans eligible dates.
It carries every queue refusal into the parent nights row. A failed queue read,
invalid queue or clock, or failed write stops retry selection before the normal
backfill scan. S717 must provide real evidence readers and test the integrated
tick before adoption; this proposal does not assert that the readers exist yet.
