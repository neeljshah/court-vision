## Floor proposal (DECISION LINE for the owner or the dispatcher)

DECISION: TRAIN_FLOOR_GAMES = ____  (proposed 20; must be fixed BEFORE seal A of the first night it applies to)

Rule: B, C and D are fitted for a scored tick only when the tick's causal history (the purged, embargoed
training set walk_forward hands to predict(), one sport per call) holds at least TRAIN_FLOOR_GAMES distinct
settled games AND both outcomes 0 and 1. Otherwise the tick is warmup: B/C/D are None on the receipt,
absent from paired_losses and cells, and counted once as model_warmup:below_game_floor or
model_warmup:one_outcome (model_warmup:no_history when the purged set is empty).

Reasoning for 20:
- The label is the game outcome, so the effective sample of a fit is the number of games, not ticks
  (ticks inside a game share one outcome). C for MLB fits an intercept, mid and eleven state features;
  NFL C fits mid and eight. Below about twice the coefficient count in games, the ridge fit is driven by
  the 1e-3 penalty and the few outcomes it has seen.
- 2 is the smallest floor that removes the S744 defect (one game) and the both-outcome rule removes the
  all-one-outcome case at any floor; but 2 to 10 games still give near-degenerate fits.
- 30 equals the per-cell verdict floor (contrast_verdict n_games 30). Using it would keep B and C
  warmup until a verdict could exist at all; that is defensible but withholds every B/C number for
  the rest of the MLB season on the evidence below.
- 20 is the proposal: above twice the largest C coefficient count served today, below the verdict floor.
  The verdict floor is NOT moved by this row.
