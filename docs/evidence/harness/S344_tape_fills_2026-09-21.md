Input contract and assumptions:
- Quotes are complete order records with unique quote_id, ticker, game_id,
  side in {yes,no}, price in integer cents, positive whole qty, submit_ts,
  optional cancel_ts, and optional replaces naming the prior quote_id.
  These represent resting bids on the named outcome, matching the existing
  paper_maker.py:131 side convention. A replacement has a new identifier,
  full new quantity and independent queue state; it cancels its predecessor
  at its submit timestamp plus cancel latency. No quantity is carried over.
- AMENDMENT 1 supersedes the prior whole-count input restriction. Explicit
  from_archive_trade and from_s341_trade adapters in tape_fill_adapters.py
  return NormalizedPrint or Refusal(reason, trade_id). Raw dictionaries are
  unscored by the core. Archive probability price describes YES; the adapter
  maps its complement for the resting NO side. S341 uses the explicit resting
  side price, preferring *_dollars and count_fp when present. Off-cent prices
  beyond the specified tolerance are refused. Venue time alone is retained.
- Print, queue, remaining and filled sizes use Decimal internally. All returned
  size fields, including remaining qty and queue metadata, are Decimal strings.
  Own order qty must be a positive whole count.
- Times are epoch seconds or timezone-aware ISO strings. ASSUMPTION: the
  active interval excludes arrival and effective cancellation. Prints must
  occur strictly after arrival. Equal-time prints sort by str(trade_id);
  duplicate identifiers retain the first successfully normalized occurrence.
  Refused rows are unscored and never consume an identifier.
- Books have ticker, ts, and yes/no lists of [price_cents, displayed_count].
  The last snapshot at or before submission supplies the queue. ASSUMPTION:
  tied latest snapshots use the largest parseable displayed size at our price.
  Any tied snapshot with an absent level, absent side, or malformed size
  makes the queue unknown; an absent snapshot also means unknown. Explicit
  displayed zero is allowed. Future books and later cancellations do not
  reduce the initial queue. Explicit tape adapters are pure; no live service is introduced.
- ASSUMPTION: unknown_queue_contracts defaults to 1,000,000; an override must
  be a positive count. This is a pessimistic modeling constant, not a
  measured queue. submit_latency_s and cancel_latency_s default to zero;
  these are unmeasured ASSUMPTIONS and must be supplied for latency studies.
- ASSUMPTION: competing orders receive price priority, then arrival priority,
  then lexical quote_id priority. Queue depletion and our fills share a
  print's finite volume budget. Same-price quotes observe the same external
  queue depletion; shared external volume is charged once per print, with
  incremental charges for larger remaining queue estimates. Higher-price
  fills consume volume before the touch queue. Our earlier same-price fills
  cannot count toward external queue depletion. No cancellation credit is inferred.
- Strict-through prints bypass the queue and fill at our resting limit;
  they do not deplete its queue for future touch prints. Touch prints first
  consume queue ahead. Missing/invalid size, side, identity, time, ticker or
  relevant price is counted unscored; duplicate rows are counted separately.
  Diagnostics set all_prints_unscored=true for nonempty input with no scored
  prints, including unconverted archive fields. Empty tape leaves it false.
- Fee source: scripts/platformkit/execution/venue_fees.py:122
  fee_kalshi_maker calls the canonical whole-order formula. The version label
  venue_fees:kalshi-maker:2026-09-01:0.0175 names the inherited implementation,
  not an independent schedule verification. Each fill's fee_units is the
  increment in cumulative order fee; partial fills together pay one rounded
  whole-order fee on filled quantity. Replacement orders round separately.
  The fee function receives the cumulative filled size as a possibly fractional
  Decimal. venue_fees._count coerces it using float(size) and abs(n); it does
  not truncate to an integer. Fee arithmetic then uses floats and the inherited
  cent ceiling; simulator size accounting remains Decimal.
