# GATE A0 2026-09-14 -- Q01: in-game score vs market

## Purpose
Reproduce the Q01 premise as a landed row: is the in-game model closer to the
outcome than the contemporaneous market price, on paired ticks, with a
game-clustered CI. Decides whether Gate A needs new capture for MLB/soccer.

## Data
`data/cache/ingame_grade_joined/mlb_clean/*.jsonl`: 227 files, 78,986 rows.
`data/cache/ingame_grade_joined/soccer_intl/*.jsonl`: 51 files, 9,003 rows.
Fields: sport, game_id, ts, model_prob, market_prob, side, state_summary,
outcome, close_ts, close_prob, edge_claimed. Both counts reproduced before
computing anything else (matches the QUEUE_2026-09-14 premise).

## Method
Paired Brier and log-loss (model minus market) on identical ticks; CI =
game-clustered bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, seed 13; 10-bin ECE both sides;
splits by phase (parsed from state_summary), market_prob band, and
minutes_to_close = close_ts - ts; tick-level change-rate for model_prob /
market_prob / state_summary. Verdict per cell: AHEAD (CI excludes 0, model
favoured), BEHIND (CI excludes 0, market favoured), UNDERPOWERED (n_games < 30
or CI contains 0). Thresholds are constants at the top of
`scripts/platformkit/ingame/gate_a0_ingame_vs_market.py`, byte-identical to
QUEUE_2026-09-14 Q01.

## By split
MLB: BEHIND in every phase bucket and in the >60-minute close bucket; the
0.6-0.8 / >0.8 price bands and every < 60-minute close bucket are UNDERPOWERED
(CI contains 0, all n_games >= 30). Soccer: BEHIND on the <0.2 / 0.2-0.4 price
bands and on every minutes_to_close bucket; all three phase buckets and the
0.4-0.6 / 0.6-0.8 / >0.8 price bands are UNDERPOWERED (n_games < 30).

## Verdict
mlb_clean: BEHIND overall. soccer_intl: BEHIND overall. Neither corpus has an
AHEAD cell anywhere. model_prob changes on 8.3 pct of MLB ticks and 36.4 pct
of soccer ticks; market_prob changes on 25.0 pct (MLB) / 36.9 pct (soccer).
