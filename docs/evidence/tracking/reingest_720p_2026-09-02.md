# G27 re-ingest audit - 2026-09-02

## Scope and method

The pod read-only census found 25 resident 360p files in
`data/footage_corpus/`; all are 960-second clips. The daemon ledger records
the clip IDs and tracking result but has no URL or source-section field. Each
YouTube source below is therefore the canonical URL recovered from the video
ID embedded in the clip filename. No cookie material was read, printed, or
committed.

The requested re-ingest window was the first 600 seconds of the bridge's
planned section. Every recoverable source here planned `*00:10:00-00:20:00`.
Downloads were serial, local, cookie-backed and section-only. A first bridge
run was stopped when its existing native-full-download fallback appeared; it
did not complete a full file. The follow-up runs did not permit a full-file
fallback.

## Visual verification

Each successful 1080p variant has six evenly spaced frames in
`reingest_720p_2026-09-02/<clip>_1080p_grid.jpg`. Its 360p counterpart is in
`reingest_720p_2026-09-02/old_360/<clip>_grid.jpg`. All 12 grids were viewed.
For every successful KBO row, the paired grids show the same panel program,
speaker shots, and graphics at matching positions. They verify source/content
identity, but also verify that these clips are not field-broadcast footage.

## What is not verified

- No high-resolution variant was pushed to the pod bridge: all six successful
  fetches were wrong-footage co-streams, and the other 13 failed closed before
  a usable section was produced.
- The 13 failed sources have not been visually compared at high resolution,
  because there is no high-resolution local file.
- The current cookie session did not expose the expected HLS itags 300/301.
  The 720p DASH candidate for `mlb_5IA4jaKNOYg` fails at ffmpeg with HTTP 403
  during section extraction. Resolving this requires a refreshed session or a
  verified HLS client path; no full-file fallback was used.
- Football was intentionally not downloaded or rendered because its lane is
  PAUSED.
