## Binding premise and section rule

pod ledger, then reads one declared tracking table at a time. It reports the
whole-set evaluated-frame distribution (min, p10, median, p90, max, n) and

## Adapter rule

`production_schema_adapter.py` accepts production player rows with
`x_position`/`y_position`, adds canonical `cls`, `frame`, `track_id`, `x`, and
`y`, and preserves supplied columns without mutation or rename. It emits one
ball row per frame only when the selected embedded coordinates are finite. When
the px embedded fields exist they supply the ball coordinates and stamp
`ball_source=embedded_px`; otherwise finite court embedded coordinates stamp
`ball_source=embedded_court`. Every output row has `scorable=False`.

Frame size is sealed: a valid supplied sidecar size is `frame_size_source`
`sidecar`; otherwise a positive finite median of `x_position / x_norm` and
`y_position / y_norm` is `estimated`; otherwise it is `absent`. Estimated or
absent size makes every size-dependent image gate NOT_APPLICABLE with reason
`frame_size_absent`; only a valid sidecar enables containment.
