# G330 preregistration -- panorama cache identity and registration proxy

Spec: `docs/evidence/tracking/specs/G330_spec.md` (worktree a1, master `6097645ed`).
Sealed BEFORE any measurement, committed ALONE. Pod is READ-ONLY (`ssh cat` / `scp` down only;
the running daemon and guard are never signalled). Build and proxies run LOCALLY, CPU only.

Written after listing the pod corpus filenames and the panorama filenames (paths only, so the
selection rule below is feasible and deterministic) and after reading `unified_pipeline.py`.
No panorama digest, no proxy and no arm had been run when this file was sealed.

## 1. Census definition (the denominator)

Every regular file whose basename matches `*pano*` or `*panorama*`, case-insensitive, under:

  POD    `/workspace/nba-ai-system` (whole tree, `find ... -type f`)
  LOCAL  the a1 worktree: `data/**` and `resources/**`

For each file record: side, path, bytes, mtime, sha256 of the raw bytes, and the video stem the
path claims (`pano_<stem>.png` gives `<stem>`; a path claiming no video is recorded as `-`).
This is EXHAUSTIVE over those trees, not a sample.

Grouping: by sha256. Report every group of size greater than one together with the distinct claimed
stems in it.

PREMISE GATE (binding, step 0): if no two panorama files that claim DIFFERENT videos share a
sha256, the premise is FALSE, the row stops, the memo says PREMISE FALSE, and no arm is run.

## 2. The three sections -- selection rule, fixed before running

From `ls /workspace/nba-ai-system/data/footage_corpus/` sorted ascending under `LC_ALL=C`, take the
distinct game-id prefixes (basename with `_s<digits>.mp4` removed) in first-appearance order; for
each of the first THREE distinct prefixes take that prefix's lexicographically-first `.mp4`.
Applied to the listing already taken, the three sections are, and will not be changed:

  S1  nba__0022400909_s1588.mp4
  S2  nba__0022401156_s2491.mp4
  S3  nba__0022401198_s2784.mp4

Three different game ids, so "different broadcasts" holds by construction; `ffprobe` width, height
and frame count are printed for each as a recorded property, not as a selection input. Each is
copied down read-only, kept under about 35 MB, and DELETED at the end. Never committed.

## 3. The two arms -- one difference only

Identical for both arms: the section, the frame indices, the stride, the detector and its output,
the matcher code path, and every constant imported live from `src/pipeline/unified_pipeline.py`
(`_SIFT_SCALE`, `_H_MIN_INLIERS`, `TOPCUT`, the shared `FLANN` matcher, the Lowe ratio `0.7`, the
bottom-seventy-percent region of interest, `cv2.findHomography(..., cv2.RANSAC, 5.0)`).

The ONLY difference is the panorama image handed to SIFT as the reference (hence `kp1` and `des1`):

  ARM F  the panorama the pod route actually used for that section: the pod's
         `resources/panos/pano_<stem>.png`; if that stem has no such file, the pod's
         `resources/pano_enhanced.png`. Downloaded byte for byte; sha256 recorded.
  ARM V  a panorama built from THAT SECTION's own frames by the route's own builder logic
         (`_scan_and_build_pano`: first gameplay frame by the route's person gate, then the route's
         five-second stitch window, `src.tracking.rectify_court.collage`, the route's wide-crop and
         `_pano_valid` gate). `_RESOURCES` is redirected at runtime to a scratch directory so the
         builder writes nothing into the repo or into `data/`.

Pre-registered ARM V rule: if the route's validity gate (`_pano_valid`: width at least 2000 and a
width-over-height ratio between 3.0 and 50.0) rejects the stitched result, ARM V is recorded
NOT BUILDABLE for that section and the row does NOT apply the builder's general-fallback
substitution (`unified_pipeline.py` lines 975-981), because that substitution would make ARM V
byte-identical to ARM F and destroy the single difference. NOT BUILDABLE is reported, never dropped.

No threshold is touched. The ratio gate, `_H_MIN_INLIERS` and every other constant are read, never set.

## 4. Proxies and their denominators

Frames evaluated per section: indices 0, 90, 180, and so on (stride 90, which is three seconds at
thirty frames per second), taking the first 40 that decode. `n_frames` is how many decoded; it is the
denominator of P1, P2 and P3 and is reported in every cell.

  P1 good SIFT matches: matches surviving the route's Lowe ratio, summed over the evaluated frames.
     Reported as a total with `n_frames`.
  P2 RANSAC inliers: `mask.sum()` summed over the evaluated frames, with `n_frames`. The inlier
     ratio is reported as the fraction of the P2 total over the P1 total.
  P3 frames with a valid homography: frames where the arm returned a matrix AND the inlier count was
     at least `_H_MIN_INLIERS`. Reported as a fraction, valid over `n_frames`.
  P4 feet inside the court polygon (the G03 quantity): the route's detector is run ONCE per frame and
     its feet points are shared by both arms. A foot is evaluated only in a frame where THAT arm
     produced a valid homography. The court point is `M1 @ (M @ [x, y, 1])` exactly as
     `unified_pipeline.py` line 3084 applies it, with `M1` from `resources/Rectify1.npy` and the map
     size from the route's `_build_court`. Inside means the projected u is at least zero and below
     the map width and the projected v is at least zero and below the map height. Reported as a
     fraction, inside over feet evaluated. The denominators differ between arms because the
     valid-homography frame sets differ; both numerator and denominator are printed for each arm, and
     the total feet detected over all evaluated frames is printed once per section.

Every integer cell in the CSV files is zero-padded to six digits; every share is written as a fraction.

## 5. Sign convention, and its stated confound

Higher is consistent with better registration for all four proxies: more good matches, more inliers,
a higher inlier ratio, a larger valid-homography share, a larger inside-the-court share.

Consistency is NOT evidence of correctness. No ground-truth court landmarks are labelled anywhere in
this row and none are created; the eye check is NONE. Inlier counts measure agreement between a frame
and a panorama, not agreement between a frame and the court.

Stated in advance, not discovered: P4 is confounded for ARM V. `M1` (`Rectify1.npy`) is calibrated for
the general fallback panorama's coordinate space (`unified_pipeline.py` lines 885 to 888), so for a
per-section panorama P4 measures the arm together with a mismatched `M1`, not registration alone. A
low ARM V P4 is therefore expected and is reported as such, never read as ARM V being worse.

Three sections are a screening sample, not the programme. A section whose ARM V will not build is a
limit of the builder on broadcast footage; it is not proof that the shared panorama is right.

## 7. No bar here is a quality bar

None of the numbers in this row is a threshold and nothing passes or fails on one. The row names a
cause from the producer code and measures agreement; it ships no fix, changes no `src/` file, moves
no threshold, and makes no claim about tracking quality. Any code fix is written only as a PROPOSED
diff under `docs/research/organization-sprint/`.
