## Corrected cited geometry

- FIBA `restricted_radius` is 1.30 m, not 1.25 m: FIBA Official Basketball Rules
  2024, Rule 2.5.7, https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-fiba-official-rules-2024-v10a.pdf.
- NBA, WNBA, and NCAA basket centres are 5.25 ft from the baseline. Their existing
  `basket_offset=4.0` remains additive and means the backboard face, never centre.
- NCAA `corner_sideline_offset` is 3.385416667 ft = 25 - (21 + 7.375/12), using
  the stated 21 ft 7.375 in corner distance. It is not a Pythagorean baseline-point solve.

## Population and independent scoring

The acceptance matrices are exactly the spec population: 200 frames per true template
per arm. The selector pools five consecutive frames into 40 disjoint decisions; each
decision has at least five frames and at least two shot IDs. The decision winner labels
its five constituent frames, so each confusion row sums to 200. The decision population
is 40 per template per arm, satisfying the additional n >= 30 decision requirement.

Each observed frame is partitioned by whole markings into disjoint FIT and VALIDATION
subsets. Non-true-template refit selection and selector hypothesis selection use FIT
only. All stored residual costs use VALIDATION only; no marking is reused across those
steps. Archive outputs include the held-out polylines and decision-level FIT costs,
bootstrap wins, distinctive-group count, and VALIDATION costs.

Unchanged thresholds: winner cost <= 0.8x runner-up; at least five frames from at least
two shots; at least two distinctive groups; same winner in at least 90 of 100 bootstraps.
Unchanged observations: Gaussian sigma Uniform[1,3] px and one contiguous 30 percent
arc-length occlusion per stroke. Arm B remains independent uniform corner jitter in
[-2,2] px and is reported, not gated.
