# G112 source-access notes

These links support source-access classifications only; they do not establish a
geometric result.

- Veo says an admin/editor must make a recording public before sharing it; a
  viewer with that link needs no Veo account, but is view-only and cannot
  download the recording. Source: [Veo sharing help](https://support.veo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4451490513169-How-to-share-Veo-recordings-on-social-media-or-via-link).
- NFL says All-22 coaches film is included with NFL+ Premium, so this is a
  subscription source rather than a plain public video. Source: [NFL plan
  support](https://support.nfl.com/hc/en-us/articles/39796576862996-Which-NFL-plan-is-right-for-me).
- Hudl's organizational terms describe sharing practice/public game video with
  organization-designated recipients; no uncredentialed full-film catalogue is
  assumed. Source: [Hudl organization terms](https://static.hudl.com/craft/legal/Organization-Terms-of-Service_2025-05-02_2.pdf).
- GameChanger permits an `Anyone` live-stream audience and a browser share
  link, but its recorded full-event viewing can depend on subscription and
  team privacy. Sources: [web live-stream
  access](https://help.gc.com/hc/en-us/articles/4946527969549-Watching-Live-Streams-on-the-Web)
  and [full-event access](https://help.gc.com/hc/en-us/articles/360057524931-Viewing-a-Live-Video-Stream).
- MLB describes MLB.TV as a subscription for live/on-demand games; that does
  not supply a public, continuous overhead/whole-infield feed. Source:
  [MLB.TV packages](https://www.mlb.com/live-stream-games/subscribe/allpackages).
- The professional soccer-analysis option is rights-managed: Wyscout pricing
  exposes paid video-view limits and its service terms prohibit publishing
  downloaded video. Sources: [Wyscout pricing](https://www.hudl.com/products/wyscout/pricing)
  and [service terms](https://get.wyscout.com/service.pdf).
