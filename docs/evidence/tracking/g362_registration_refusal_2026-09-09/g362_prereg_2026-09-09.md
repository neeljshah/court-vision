## 3. FIT / VALIDATION partition of whole marking strokes (the core of this row)

A MARKING STROKE is one physical marking as observed: a collinear group returned by
`line_calibration.candidate_line_group_details(segments, angle_deg=5.0, offset_px=18.0)` over the
G334 detected segments, retained when its observed extent is at least MIN_STROKE_PX = 40.0 px.
Strokes are whole; a stroke is NEVER split across FIT and VALIDATION. This is the defect the row
targets: G334's `heldout_split` permutes individual LSD SEGMENTS, so fragments of one physical line
land on both sides and every held-out residual is self-fit contaminated (contract B8).

STROKE ID: the first 16 hex characters of the sha256 of the ASCII string
`"G362|%.4f|%.4f|%.4f|%.1f|%.1f" % (a, b, c, extent_lo, extent_hi)` where (a, b, c) is the fitted
line normalised so that hypot(a, b) == 1.0 and c >= 0.0 (sign convention, section 8), and the
extents are the observed support extent along the fitted direction. STROKE DIGEST: the sha256 of
the same string, printed in full in `strokes.csv`. Ids are content-addressed, so the partition is
reproducible from the archived CSV without re-running the detector.

FAMILY: the 15-degree orientation bin of the fitted line, CENTRED on multiples of 15 degrees and
wrapped: `int(round(theta_deg / 15.0)) % 12` on `theta_deg = degrees(atan2(-a, b)) % 180.0`. A
MARKING FAMILY is one occupied bin. Centred rather than starting at zero because the direction wraps
at 0/180: with bins starting at zero, the two anti-aliased sides of ONE painted marking fit at 179.9
and 0.1 degrees, land in different bins, and one physical orientation would satisfy the two-family
bar on its own. The bar is meant to require two DIFFERENT markings' orientations.

FIXED SUPPORT DENSITY: each stroke contributes support points sampled along its fitted line at
exactly SUPPORT_SPACING_PX = 6.0 px between its extent endpoints, plus both endpoints. The count is
therefore a function of the stroke's LENGTH ALONE and cannot be raised by lowering an LSD threshold
or by detecting more fragments of the same marking. This closes G334's measured defect (2): the
route's forward error there fell from 24.811 px to 14.811 px on one section purely because a denser
held-out set was supplied, and the metric moved without the map moving.

PARTITION RULE (deterministic, no seed, no frame content): within each family, sort the strokes by
stroke id ascending; reserve the first `ceil(len(family) / 4)` for VALIDATION. Over all families
this reserves >= 25 pct of strokes by construction, and it reserves at least one stroke from every
family that has any. The remaining strokes are FIT.

FIT ALONE chooses H: the hypothesis enumeration, the template, the search, the soft-chamfer
distance field and the runner-up ranking see the FIT strokes' segments and nothing else. The
VALIDATION strokes make ONE accept/refuse decision on the FROZEN winner. There is never a refit,
never a second candidate, never a re-partition, and never a per-frame parameter choice.

THE FIT-SIDE OBJECTIVE, sealed here. The search is G334's complete structured enumeration
(`enumerate_hypotheses`, no random draw) with two rules carried forward from G352's own
preregistration, reimplemented on the landed G334 primitives because the G352 module is not landed
on master (section 0):

  (i)  WHOLE-TEMPLATE SCORE. A hypothesis scores the soft-chamfer sum over the template points that
       fall inside the image, DIVIDED BY THE WHOLE 398-point template. An out-of-frame point
       contributes zero to the sum and stays in the denominator, so a fit that keeps a high mean by
       throwing most of the court out of the image ranks below the truth instead of above it. This
       is G334's named defect (1); the landed surviving-points objective is used only by the RAW
       negative control of section 5(a), where reproducing the archived behaviour is the point.
  (ii) THE VALIDITY GATE RUNS INSIDE THE SEARCH. The argmax is taken over GATE-VALID hypotheses
       only, using `g334_court_line_calibration.gate` unchanged and with its sealed G334 constants
       untouched, rather than applying the gate to the winner alone. The gate is evaluated only on
       candidates that beat the best valid score so far, which is exact: a candidate at or below
       the incumbent cannot become the argmax.

Neither rule is a bar and neither moves one; both are objective definitions fixed before any
broadcast frame is scored.

## 4. States and validity bars (sealed; the bar is never lowered, only reported unmet)

Exactly four states, decided in this order:

  NO_LINES      -- fewer than 2 marking families, or fewer than 4 strokes, are extracted at all.
  NO_VALIDATION -- the partition reserves fewer than MIN_VAL_FAMILIES = 2 held-out families, or
                   fewer than MIN_VAL_POINTS = 30 held-out support points, in this frame.
                   ABSENT SUPPORTS ARE NEVER VALID and are never counted as a refusal either.
  REFUSED       -- validation was possible and the frozen winner did not clear the bar below, or
                   the G334 gate itself rejected the winner (`too_few_groups`, `no_valid_h`,
                   `implausible_scale`). The rejecting clause is recorded.
  VALID         -- validation was possible and the frozen winner cleared the bar below.

BAR (bidirectional, on VALIDATION support points only, at 720p; a frame of another height is scaled
to 720 rows before any residual is taken, and the scale factor is archived):

  forward_median = median over the in-image projected template points of the distance to the
                   nearest VALIDATION support point;
  inverse_median = median over the VALIDATION support points of the distance to the nearest
                   in-image projected template point;
  VALID requires  max(forward_median, inverse_median) <= 8.0 px
                  AND held-out families >= 2 AND held-out support points >= 30.

Both directions are required because either alone is buyable: a fit that projects most of the
template out of frame has few forward points, and a fit that floods the image has a small inverse
distance. Out-of-frame template points are NOT dropped from the denominator: the forward median is
taken over the whole 398-point template with an out-of-frame point charged the sealed penalty
distance PENALTY_PX = 64.0 px. This is the whole-template denominator G352 preregistered, carried
forward unchanged.

(a) HISTORICAL. The RAW fitter (`g334_court_line_calibration.fit_frame` with its output gate
    omitted, so the argmax is returned whatever the gate thinks) AND the FULL CASCADE (the G362
    `decide` of section 3 and 4) both face the 84 archived cells of section 1, if the pixels survive
    or can be re-fetched by G361 identity. If they cannot, this arm is reported NOT VERIFIED with
    the reason, and never silently omitted (A7).

(b) FRESH BLIND. >= 200 non-court frames from >= 30 sections, EVEN SAMPLE: within each section, the
    frame indices are `floor((k + 0.5) * n_decodable / m)` for k in 0..m-1 with
    m = ceil(200 / n_sections), which is an interior sample and never a head slice (A3, B7). The
    sections are selected BLIND -- by source identity only, before any frame of them is decoded --
    and their non-court character is established by an eye check of an even contact sheet committed
    BEFORE the decisions are scored. No frame of any section used in section 6's positives appears
    here.

BAR ON NEGATIVES: 0 accepts of 84 historical (or NOT VERIFIED) AND 0 accepts of >= 200 fresh, for
the CASCADE. The RAW fitter is expected to accept and is reported for contrast, never as a pass or
a fail: it is the control that shows the refusal comes from the held-out decision and not from a
weaker detector.

`negative_decisions.csv` carries one row per (arm, section, frame): source key, video_sha256,

## 8. Sign and normalisation conventions

Distances are non-negative pixels at 720p. A homography named `image` maps feet to image pixels and
a homography named `court` maps image pixels to feet; both are normalised by their (2,2) element,
which is asserted non-zero. Line coefficients (a, b, c) are normalised to hypot(a, b) == 1.0 with
c >= 0.0, negating all three when c < 0.0, so a line and its reverse share one stroke id. Any
displayed improvement delta is baseline loss minus candidate loss, positive meaning the candidate is
better; this row's headline quantities are counts and residual medians, not paired losses, so
walk-forward, purging and embargo are not applicable and no differential series is claimed.

## 11. Determinism

No random draw exists anywhere in this row: the G334 search is a complete enumeration, the partition
is a sort by content-addressed stroke id, and the sampling rules of sections 5 and 6 are closed-form
index formulas. `cv2.setNumThreads(1)` and `OMP_NUM_THREADS=2` are set inside the modules. The
bootstrap of section 6 uses `numpy.random.default_rng` seeded from the frame's own stroke-id digest,
so it reproduces from the archived CSV alone.
