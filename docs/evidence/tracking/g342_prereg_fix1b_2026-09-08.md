Amendments (fix 1b), sealed here:

1. Pseudo-shot grouping. The 200 independently sampled renders per template are generated
   in a fixed order and labelled frame_id 0..199 (shared between arm a and arm b: both arms
   score the same 200 observed frames, arm b under the corner-jittered H per the original
   prereg). shot_id = frame_id // 5, giving 40 pseudo-shots of 5 frames each. A decision
   pools exactly 2 consecutive pseudo-shots (shots 2d and 2d+1, d = 0..19): 10 frames, 2
   distinct shot_id values, meeting select_template's own >= 5-frame / >= 2-shot
   precondition without padding or frame reuse across decisions. This yields 20 independent,
   non-overlapping decisions per template per arm, consuming all 200 frames exactly once.
   Each decision is scored by calling `select_template(frames, templates, homographies)`
   directly -- the sealed selector at its own default seed, not a reimplementation -- so the
   >= 5-frame, >= 2-shot, distinctive-group and 90/100 bootstrap gates all run for real. The
   acceptance bar (true-win >= 0.95, wrong-winner <= 0.02) is evaluated over these n = 20
   gated decisions per template per arm, not over the 200 raw frames; that n = 20 is smaller
   than the original per-frame n = 200 is carried into the memo's NOT VERIFIED as a reduced-
   power caveat.
2. Symmetric distance. `_distance(a, b)` in template_select.py is redefined as the mean of
   the two one-directional median-nearest-point distances:
   `symmetric(a, b) = 0.5 * (directed(a, b) + directed(b, a))`, where `directed(x, y)` is the
   prior (now internal, one-directional) per-point-of-x nearest-neighbour-in-y median. This
   replaces the directional `_distance` used both for the per-stroke cost and the 4 px
   unsupported-stroke penalty; no other formula in template_select.py changes.
3. Per-frame archive. For every (arm, true_template, frame_id, scored_template) quadruple --
   4 true templates x 200 frames x 2 arms x 4 scored templates = 6400 rows -- archive.csv
   records the scored template's 3x3 homography (row-major h00..h22, rounded to 6 decimal
   places) and its per-frame cost against that frame's observed segments. The generating
   (pre-noise, pre-refit, pre-jitter) H for the true template on arm a (scored_template ==
   true_template, arm == "a") is exactly the sampled camera H for that frame_id; H uniqueness
   (sealed at 800/800 in the rejected run) is recomputable by de-duplicating those 800 rows
   alone, without rerunning the driver.
4. Unchanged from the rejected candidate: the arm B corner-jitter method, the best-of-50
   non-true-template re-fit method, and the pairwise separation metric (NBA vs
   WNBA/FIBA/NCAA, cost ratio <= 0.8x, on the 200 NBA-true frames per arm) -- spec:37-53
   describes pairwise separation as a frame-level cost share, a diagnostic distinct from the
   selector's gated decision, and arm B's jitter method was not challenged by the verifier.
