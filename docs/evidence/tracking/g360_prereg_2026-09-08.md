## Scope and premise

This is a court-presence screening validation, not a geometric-validity or
downstream calibration claim. Before any held-out selection, evaluate the 22
unique G350 adjudicated units with USABLE_WIDE as the positive reference and
all other labels as negative. Decode each named representative frame from its
recorded source one source at a time. The premise computes rank AUC separately
for content-gate basketball surface fraction and the integer line-family count.
If neither AUC is at least 0.75, write PREMISE FALSE and do not select, rate, or
score a held-out set.

## Cue and sealed rule

`court_presence(frame)` converts one BGR frame to grayscale, applies Canny
(50,150), and uses HoughLinesP with threshold 32, minLineLength 8 percent of
frame width, and maxLineGap 12. A family is a 15-degree orientation bin with a
long segment; a valid line cue has at least two occupied bins. Surface is the
landed content gate's basketball HSV surface fraction, read without modifying
that gate. The composite score is the mean of clipped surface/0.12 and
families/2 terms.

The held-out court decision is fixed from the premise only: enumerate
`families >= F AND surface >= S` over observed premise F and S values, retain
rules predicting at least one positive and one negative, and select the rule
with maximal Youden J; ties resolve by greater precision, then greater recall,
then greater F, then greater S. Record F and S in `premise.csv`. No threshold,
algorithm parameter, input list, or selection order changes after this seal.

## Held-out selection and blind reference

The population is G350 candidates plus post-epoch clips. Enumerate sections
and sample frame positions at evenly spaced interior fractions, never from a
head slice. Select at least 200 unique representative frames across at least
20 sections, 10 games, and 3 competitions, retaining at least 60 predicted
COURT and 60 predicted NON_COURT; remaining selections are from ABSTAIN in
SHA-256 order on `G360-2026-09-08|frame_key`. Each held-out record names full
path, video SHA-256, byte size, native dimensions, section, game, competition,
and frame index. It is sealed before rating.

Sheets contain a representative frame and three-frame strip plus only a frame
identity. Terra and Sol independently use exactly USABLE_COURT, CLOSEUP,
CROWD_GRAPHICS, UNKNOWN with a one-line reason. Equal labels are the reference;
disagreements go to a blind adjudicator. Cohen kappa is computed on the primary
four-label series. Sheets contain no cue value or prediction.

## Score and bar

Positive reference is USABLE_COURT; predicted COURT is positive. Report the
complete confusion table, Wilson 95 percent intervals for precision(COURT) and
recall(USABLE_COURT), abstention share, predicted-class counts, and an additive
per-mille per-competition breakdown. The bar is byte-identical to the spec:
precision >= 0.90 and recall >= 0.80 on at least 200 rated frames, with at
least 30 frames in each predicted class. Otherwise verdict is NOT VALIDATED.
Delta convention for any displayed delta is baseline loss minus candidate loss;
positive means candidate better. This static classification audit has no paired
loss comparison, so walk-forward/CPCV, purge, embargo, evaluator states, and
archived differential losses are not applicable.

## Fixed test

The focused synthetic test asserts a two-family court frame scores above a
synthetic crowd frame and that the sheet builder emits no cue value or
prediction. It is not a corpus score.
