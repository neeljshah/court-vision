# G371 truth-free symmetry-margin preregistration

## Scope

Synthetic CPU-only selector repair. The route imports the sealed G367 K=7/M=8 enumeration from `g367_search._candidates`; it never calls `g367_search.search`.

## Fixed selector

- Candidate-class tolerance: 2.0 px at 720p, over `TEMPLATE_POINTS`, using `g367_symmetry.equivalent` and `GROUP`.
- Enumeration: K=7 groups per family, M=8 refined hypotheses; counts are emitted as `candidate_hypotheses_count` and `deduplicated_symmetry_images_count`.
- Representative objective J: `g362_fit_validate.whole_template_score` evaluated only on the FIT-stroke distance field after `g365_refine_b.refine_b` over FIT supports.
- J ordering is descending; ties use the original enumeration rank, then source element, then the rounded 3x3 matrix tuple.
- Margin epsilon: 1e-12. Margin is `(J2-J1)/max(abs(J1), epsilon)` after converting the descending objective difference to the non-negative `J1-J2` form.
- Acceptance margin: 0.10. A distinct class with margin below 0.10 is `REFUSED_MARGIN`; absent distinct class is `NO_DISTINCT_RUNNER_UP`.
- Held-out decision: G362's unchanged validation median ceiling of 8.0 px; it is evaluated after the truth-free selection only.
- `geometry_status` is independent of `orientation_status`; the latter is always `UNKNOWN` because the line route contains no orientation cue.

## Labels and controls

- Labelled and modulo gaps are evaluation only. The declared modulo definition is `form_two`; every CSV with a modulo gap writes `modulo_form=form_two`.
- Exact control compares an identical known-H pair and must be 0.000000 px under form two.
- Candidate-order permutation and cloned candidates must retain the selected class, winner J, runner-up J, and margin exactly.
- Planted distinct classes have a second-class J within the 0.10 margin band and must be refused. Seeds are 3710909 through 3710938 inclusive.
- Noise sweep uses geometries G1_SYNTH_QUAD/G2_WIDE/G3_TIGHT/G4_OFF_AXIS, sigma 0.25/0.50/1.00 px, draws 0..29, and seed `sha256('G371|geometry|sigma|draw')[:16]` as an unsigned hexadecimal integer.
- Sensitivity probe feet are the fixed 17x11 court grid from x=0..50 and y=0..94 feet; report median and p95 metres against 0.25 m and 0.50 m budgets, respectively.
