## Scheduling change and reproduction

- Before: `JOB_TIMEOUT_SECONDS = 3,600` s; the preceding budget was 2,700 s.
- After: `JOB_TIMEOUT_SECONDS = 12,000` s in
  `scripts/platformkit/track_daemon.py`.
- Provenance: the temporary 12,000 s budget is roughly 1.37x the largest
  healthy 8,773 s pre-budget completion and will be replaced with a per-clip
  frame-derived budget once G56 has denominator-bearing ledger rows.
- The headroom is deliberate: the pre-budget sample contains completed jobs,
  so its 8,773 s maximum is a lower bound on legitimate duration, not a
  ceiling.

The pre-budget duration window is the append-only ledger before the first
2,700 s kill, as reproduced in
`docs/evidence/tracking/g55_timeout_budget_2026-09-02.md`: tennis n=6,
median 7,513.5 s, p95 8,723.5 s, and max 8,773 s. The G55 reproduction also
records the all-row pod ledger path and its 397 rows. This is the provenance
window; it is not a sample of jobs that survived a current budget.

`docs/evidence/tracking/g73_job_budget/count_would_have_been_killed.py` counts
every nonblank JSONL row with `seconds > budget_seconds`, failing if a row has
no integer `seconds` value. Its recorded all-row result is
`docs/evidence/tracking/g73_job_budget/historical_killed_count.json`:

```text
before_budget_seconds=3600
after_budget_seconds=12000
historical_rows=397
would_have_been_killed=0
```

Thus, none of the 397 historical rows would have been killed by 12,000 s. The
count was not fit to completions under the 3,600 s budget; fitting to survivors
would be circular.
