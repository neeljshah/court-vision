## The sealed rules

  is what the daemon route does. The conflict with the registered 0.3 is stated in
  the verdict line. `imgsz` and `half` are the route's own.
- **BIT-IDENTITY** between two per-frame box sets means: equal box count AND, after

  directions with its denominator. Agreement is reported SEPARATELY from

- **Every decoded frame is in every denominator, zero-box frames included.**
