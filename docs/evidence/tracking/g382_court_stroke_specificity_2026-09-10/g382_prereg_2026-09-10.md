# G382 Court Stroke Specificity Preregistration

## 1. Scope and status

This is a preparation-only protocol for G382. It is sealed before any source
frame extraction, rater marking, adjudication, extraction result, or scored
comparison. The preparation agent has not opened or rated a real frame.

The measuring finisher runs native-frame rating on the PC and frozen-stroke
extraction on the pod CPU. This protocol does not authorize a fitter, GPU work,
producer changes, feature flags, registry writes, or a deployment.

## 2. Sources and premise check

Before measurement, re-run the binding G379 condition from the committed
`docs/evidence/tracking/g379_broadcast_geometry_2026-09-10/summary.json`:
`sections_meeting_all_three == 0` and `raw_state_totals.REFUSED == 352`.
Search the tracking evidence tree for an existing quantitative painted-marking
specificity receipt. If it already supplies this protocol's two shares,
category counts, empty-frame share, reference completeness, and independent
process identity, report PREMISE FALSE with that receipt; otherwise continue.

Before dispatch, make a complete availability and dimension census from the
chosen G386 receipt or corpus census. Native pixels must cover the sealed draw;
otherwise report NOT VALIDATED without fitting or waiting for a daemon.

## 3. Sealed frame selection

Use `g382_select.py` on one receipt/census projection containing section id,
video id, frame order, and native source path. Sort by video and time ordering,
select 12 unique sections evenly across that ordering, require at least 6 video
ids, and select five strictly interior even frame ranks in each selected
section. The result is 60 unique planned native frames. Preserve every draw or
decode failure; never replace a selected item after rating starts.

`frames.csv` records section id, video id, native path, native dimensions,
frame order, selection rank, and retained/missing status. The future census
projection is committed after this seal and before its count is used.

## 4. Blind reference protocol

Two raters independently receive opaque-id sheets that are byte copies of the
full native frames, with no extractor output or score. Each traces every visible
painted straight court marking as a polygon and assigns a physical marking id.
They separately mask every pixel as PAINTED, STANDS, LED, SCORE_BUG, OTHER, or
UNREADABLE. Regions outside all masks are UNKNOWN; UNKNOWN is not discarded.

Raters write one JSON per opaque id under separate rater directories. Claude
adjudicates disagreements using source pixels only before extraction results
are exposed. Originals, final masks, disagreement rows, and unresolved regions
are retained. Agreement is reported as a process diagnostic, not truth.

## 5. Frozen extraction and resize identity

For each retained native frame, `g382_score.py` resizes pixels once to height
720 and width `round(native_width * 720 / native_height)` with OpenCV
INTER_AREA. It imports only `g362_strokes.extract_strokes` at that working
scale. The reference labels are first rasterized at native resolution and then
resized with the same target shape and INTER_NEAREST. This is the exact recorded
reference-mask transform. Painted polygons are dilated by a 5 by 5 elliptical
kernel at 720p, which is the fixed 2 px dilation.

Run the scorer in two fresh processes on the same staged native pixels. Each
run writes its own canonical receipt. `repeats.json` compares both receipts and
requires identity of frame, whole-stroke, and support-label rows before a
measurement can be complete.

## 6. Classification rule

All whole strokes emitted by the frozen extractor are scored. A stroke is
ON_MARKING when at least 80 percent of its fixed 6 px supports lie in the
2 px-dilated PAINTED polygon. A support whose base label is UNKNOWN is in the
denominator and never contributes to this numerator. The scorer also records
continuous painted-dilated support overlap for every stroke.

Every support receives exactly one base label: PAINTED, STANDS, LED,
SCORE_BUG, OTHER, UNREADABLE, or UNKNOWN. Category support totals include every
support. A no-stroke planned frame stays in `frames.csv` and the exports.

## 7. Planned outputs and reporting

The finisher writes `frames.csv`, `masks/`, `ratings.csv`, `adjudication.csv`,
`strokes.csv`, `support_labels.csv`, `marking_only.json`, `repeats.json`,
`summary.json`, `renders/`, and common receipts in the matching evidence
directory. Report on-marking strokes over all strokes, painted supports over all
supports, per-frame shares and macro mean over frames with strokes, no-stroke
frames over planned frames, reference completeness, process identity, and all
five non-marking labels. Frames are sampling units; strokes are nested.

Marking-only export is an oracle diagnostic for G383. It contains complete
whole strokes only, with physical marking ids, families, and lengths; it does
not split a physical marking and does not create an automated extractor or a
training confirmation.

## 8. Acceptance and boundaries

Complete measurement requires all 60 planned frames accounted for, at least 30
decoded/rated frames, classification of every emitted stroke including UNKNOWN,
identical independent-process results, and no fitting. There is no minimum
purity requirement. Verdict vocabulary is DONE, PARTIAL, NOT VALIDATED, or
PREMISE FALSE only. The preparation verdict is PREPARE-ONLY; no measurement
verdict is claimed here.

Do not move G362/G365/G371/G374 code, constants, threshold 0.30, producer
fields, weights, flags, or historical evidence. This work is not a predictive
comparison; no evaluator, ledger charge, calibration delta, or results ledger
row is applicable during preparation.

## 9. Verification plan

Run only `tests/platformkit/test_g382_court_stroke_specificity.py -q` during
preparation. It verifies transform roundtrip, UNKNOWN in the denominator,
no-stroke frame retention, complete physical-marking identity, and repeat
receipt identity. Its seal check reads this file, normalizes CRLF to LF, and
hashes bytes above this line rather than using a committed revision.

The finisher self-checks `docs/evidence/tracking/VERIFIER_CONTRACT.md` sections
A and B before submitting the measurement. The future memo states every input
path, byte size, resolution, machine, code identities, and the explicit
NOT VERIFIED list.
