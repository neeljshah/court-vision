## 0. Scope, sign convention, and what this row is not

This is a GEOMETRIC FITTER-PRECISION measurement on synthetic fixtures. No broadcast frame is opened
by any command in section 11. It makes no claim about detection on real footage, about downstream
tracking or ownership, and no claim of any kind about forecast quality or money; every quantity here
is a pixel distance between two homographies' images of the same court template.

Sign convention: distances are non-negative pixels at 720p. A homography named `image` maps feet to
image pixels; both matrices are normalised by their (2, 2) element, which is asserted non-zero.
IMPROVEMENT is BASELINE gap minus REFINED gap, so a POSITIVE improvement means the refined fit is
closer to the truth. A NEGATIVE improvement on any geometry is a REJECT of the candidate refinement
and is reported as such; it is never re-signed, re-scaled or dropped.

## 3. The refinement (sealed; every constant below is the one in `g365_refine.py`)

Start: the 4-corner homography H0 that the archived path already returned, normalised by H0[2, 2].
No other initialisation exists and no restart is attempted.

Parameters: 8. H is parameterised by its first eight entries with H[2, 2] fixed at 1.0.

Objective: over the FIT supports only, minimise the SYMMETRIC point-to-line residual

    forward_i = signed perpendicular distance, in IMAGE pixels, from support i to the IMAGE of the
                straight court marking assigned to it (that marking's two endpoints are projected
                through H and the line through them is normalised to hypot(a, b) == 1);
    inverse_i = signed perpendicular distance, in COURT feet, from H^-1(support i) to that same
                marking in the court plane, multiplied by the sealed weight `px_per_ft`.

`px_per_ft` = sqrt(image area of the half-court quad under H0 / 2350 square feet). It is computed
ONCE from H0 and held fixed for the whole run, so the objective is the same function at every
iteration; a per-point Jacobian weight would make the objective depend on where the supports happen
to fall. A non-invertible or non-finite candidate is charged BIG_PX = 1.0e4 per residual rather than
having its supports dropped, so no candidate can buy a small residual by discarding its own data.

Correspondence: each FIT support is assigned to the NEAREST straight marking under H0, measured to
the infinite line, and is DROPPED if that distance exceeds ASSIGN_MAX_PX. The assignment is computed
once, from H0, and held fixed; a correspondence re-cut every iteration can walk a support into a
neighbouring marking and the run stops being reproducible from the archived inputs alone.

Straight markings: the NINE two-point polylines of the landed `g334_court_template.template_polylines`
(baseline y = 0, half-court y = 47, free-throw line y = 19, sidelines x = 0 and x = 50, lane lines
x = 17 and x = 33, corner-three lines x = 3 and x = 47). The template's three ARCS are excluded: a
point-to-line residual is defined for a straight marking only.

Loss and solver: Huber, with

    HUBER_DELTA_PX = 2.0        (scipy `f_scale`)
    MAX_ITER       = 200        (scipy `max_nfev`; the cap is asserted in the test)
    TOL            = 1e-10      (scipy `xtol`, `ftol` and `gtol`)
    ASSIGN_MAX_PX  = 12.0
    MIN_ASSIGNED   = 12         (fewer assigned supports: the refinement DECLINES and returns H0
                                 unchanged with status `too_few_assigned` -- missing support is not
                                 bad support, contract B3)
    BIG_PX         = 1.0e4

OPTIMISER, checked and recorded rather than assumed: `scipy.optimize.least_squares`, method `trf`,
loss `huber`. scipy IS installed on the pod (1.18.1, alongside numpy 2.1.2 and cv2 4.14.0, recorded
into `summary.json` as `versions`), so NO hand-rolled numpy fallback exists in this row -- a second
solver that is never exercised is a second thing that can drift. `trf` performs no random draw.

A refined matrix that is non-finite or singular (|det| < 1e-12) is REFUSED: H0 is returned unchanged
with status `degenerate`. Refusing to move is a valid outcome; returning a matrix that maps the
court onto a point is not.

## 4. FIT / VALIDATION separation (contract B8)

On the GEOMETRY path the partition is G362's own `g362_strokes.partition`, unchanged: whole strokes,
content-addressed, the lowest quarter of stroke ids within each family held out. The refinement is
given `support_array(FIT strokes)` and nothing else. The VALIDATION strokes are then handed, exactly
as in G362, to `g362_fit_validate.bidirectional`, which reports the forward and inverse medians for
BOTH the baseline and the refined matrix in `recovery.csv`. G362's held-out bars are read, never
rewritten.

On the NOISE path the partition is by sealed line index: FIT = (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8),
VALIDATION = (6, 7) into `straight_template_lines()` -- that is, the free-throw line and one
corner-three line are held out. FIT covers two W-family and five L-family markings, which is what the
4-corner baseline of section 5(b) needs.

The test proves the separation two ways: the held-out support array is byte-identical before and
after the refinement, and REFINING WITH THE HELD-OUT POINTS ADDED RETURNS A DIFFERENT MATRIX, which
it could not if those points were already being read.

## 5. Controls

(a) EXACT (CONSTRUCT, contract Q7): sigma = 0.0. The nine markings' endpoints are projected through
    the known matrix with no jitter. The 4-corner baseline must reproduce the known matrix at
    0.000 px (G362's diagnostic) and the refinement must LEAVE IT THERE. Threshold: < 1e-6 px, the
    same threshold `summary.json` records as `exact_stays_zero`.

(b) NOISE SWEEP (SAMPLED): each endpoint of each of the nine markings is displaced by an independent
    N(0, sigma) draw in x and in y, sigma in {0.0, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00} px. DRAWS = 40 per (geometry,
    sigma), which is above the n >= 30 rail. Per draw the archived corner solve of section 5(b1) and
    the refinement of section 3 are BOTH run on the SAME noisy lines and both gaps are archived.

    (b1) The archived corner solve, reproduced exactly: intersect the noisy baseline (index 0) and
         half-court (index 3) lines with the noisy sidelines (indices 1 and 2) to give four corners
         in HALF_COURT_QUAD order, then ONE `cv2.getPerspectiveTransform`. No refinement.

(c) THE FOUR GEOMETRIES (section 6), on the DETECTED path, one row each in `recovery.csv`.

## 7. Bars and verdict ladder -- byte-identical to the spec, never moved (Q3, B10)

    bar 1: refined recovery gap <= 1.0 px on ALL FOUR geometries (`recovery.csv`)
    bar 2: refined median gap <= 1.0 px at sigma = 0.50 px over 40 draws, each geometry (`sweep.csv`)
    bar 3: the exact control stays < 1e-6 px before AND after the refinement
    bar 4: NO geometry worsens (improvement >= 0 on every geometry)
    also:  0 bars moved, 0 src edits, nothing written under `data/`

1.0 px is G362's bar, carried here unchanged. A bar found unmeetable is reported CLOSED AT LIMIT or
PARTIAL, naming the geometry or the sigma that failed; it is never lowered and the fixtures are not
re-cut until they pass. `summary.json` evaluates each of the four clauses SEPARATELY so a partial
result cannot be read as a whole one.

    DONE          all four bars hold
    PARTIAL       any bar unmet, naming the geometry or sigma
    REJECT        the candidate refinement worsens any geometry (bar 4) -- a REJECT of the
                  refinement, not of the row; the row still closes with its measured table
    PREMISE FALSE section 2's condition

An honest PARTIAL or REJECT is a success. No result in this row may be described in language of
money, return or advantage of any kind (Q6).

## 12. Determinism

The GEOMETRY path contains no random draw at all: G334's search is a complete enumeration and
G362's partition is a sort by content-addressed stroke id. The NOISE path's only draw is
`numpy.random.default_rng(seed)` with

    seed = int(sha256("G365|<geometry>|<sigma with 2 decimals>|<draw with 3 digits>")[:8], 16)

so every draw reproduces from `sweep.csv` alone. `cv2.setNumThreads(1)` is set inside the modules.
The unit test uses the DISJOINT prefix `G365-TEST`, so no test number can ever be a sweep number.
