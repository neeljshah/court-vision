## 1. THE ONE SEALED CUE -- `ANCHOR_SIDE_OCR`

An asymmetric venue-text anchor read by the DEPLOYED optical character route, with no line fit anywhere in it.

ROUTE. `src.tracking.scoreboard_ocr._get_reader()` imported from the READ-ONLY deploy tree
`/workspace/deploy/nba-ai-system` (PYTHONPATH). That factory returns PaddleOCR when installed and EasyOCR
otherwise; the pod has no PaddleOCR, so it returns `easyocr.Reader(["en"], gpu=False, verbose=False)` with
the cached `craft_mlt_25k` and `english_g2` weights. The reader is called as
`readtext(band, detail=1, paragraph=False)`. The confidence floor is `scoreboard_ocr._OCR_CONF_MIN`
and the top overlay fraction is `scoreboard_ocr._TOP_FRAC`, both imported, never restated and never edited.
No file under `src/` is written. The engine actually used is recorded in `summary.json`.

BAND. For a frame of height H and width W, the anchor band is rows `[int(_TOP_FRAC * H), int(0.82 * H))`,
full width. The deployed route's own top overlay strip is removed by `_TOP_FRAC`; the bottom 18 percent is
removed so that the centred broadcast score banner and the nearest spectator apron are outside the band.

DECISION RULE.
  - Keep every detection whose confidence is `>= _OCR_CONF_MIN`.
  - Weight of a kept detection = polygon area in square pixels, times its confidence.
  - A detection is LEFT when its polygon centroid x is `< W / 2`, otherwise RIGHT.
  - `left_mass` and `right_mass` are the summed weights; `total_mass = left_mass + right_mass`;
    `hi = max(left_mass, right_mass)`, `lo = min(left_mass, right_mass)`.
  - UNKNOWN when `total_mass < MASS_FLOOR`, where `MASS_FLOOR = 2000.0` square-pixel-confidence units.
  - UNKNOWN when `hi / max(lo, 1e-9) < RATIO_MARGIN`, where `RATIO_MARGIN` is imported from
    `scripts.platformkit.tracking.g367_cues` and is 1.15.
  - Otherwise LEFT when `left_mass > right_mass`, else RIGHT.
  - `confidence = hi / total_mass`, reported for every frame including UNKNOWN ones.
  - ANY missing evidence is UNKNOWN and is passed on, never quarantined (contract B3): an unreadable cache
    file, an empty band, a reader failure and a zero-detection band all emit UNKNOWN with confidence 0.0 and
    a named `unknown_reason`. No frame is ever dropped from the denominator for being UNKNOWN.
  - The rule is a pure function of ONE frame. No section-level, temporal or cross-frame information enters it.

MASS_FLOOR and the band bounds were fixed by inspecting frames from the three DEVELOPMENT games named in
section 2, which are excluded from every scored artifact of this row. That inspection is prior-seen data and
is disclosed here rather than hidden. No parameter is fitted to any rated label.

QUANTITY, and the disclosed reconciliation with the spec. The spec's METHOD 1 sentence says the cue emits a
side "for the basket the home team attacks", while its METHOD 2 sentence has the raters label "the court side
of an asymmetric anchor". A scored comparison needs ONE quantity, so this prereg seals the second: the image
half that carries the venue-text anchor. The reason is measured, not stylistic -- G367 already recorded that
attack direction alone cannot orient absolute court coordinates, and a single still frame carries no attack
direction a blind rater could label. The attack-direction quantity is therefore NOT VERIFIED by this row and
is filed as a new gap. No bar in the ACCEPTANCE RULE is touched by this choice.

## 2. SAMPLING RULE (sealed, with its seed)

SALT = `G378-2026-09-10|`. `sha(text) = sha256((SALT + text).encode("ascii"))`.
Corpus = `/workspace/data/footage_corpus`, containers named `<game>_s<offset>.mp4`; `game` is the basename
with the trailing `_s<offset>.mp4` removed. `/workspace/data` is never written.

  - Games are ordered by `sha(game)` hex ascending.
  - The LAST THREE games in that order are DEVELOPMENT and are excluded from every scored artifact:
    `basketball__fiba-tZeqmc_-_sA`, `ncaa_basketball__g7dzEFLiemQ`, `basketball__euroleague-a_GxgklBtEs`.
  - The remaining games form the EVALUATION pool.
  - Sections inside a game are ordered by `sha(section)` hex ascending, where `section` is the basename
    without extension.
  - Selection is a round robin over the evaluation games in the sealed game order, taking at most
    `MAX_PER_GAME = 2` sections per game, until `SECTIONS_TARGET = 20` sections are held.
  - A section is ELIGIBLE when its container opens and reports at least 200 frames. An ineligible section is
    replaced by the next section in the sealed order and both the exclusion and its reason are written to
    `exclusions.csv`. Every exclusion happens before any cue output and before any rating; there are no
    label-driven top-ups.
  - Ticks: with `TICKS = 12` and a section of `n` frames, `step = n // (TICKS + 1)` and the tick indices are
    `step * (i + 1)` for `i` in 0 to 11. Every tick is strictly interior, the spacing is even, and no head
    slice is possible (contract B7, A3).
  - Target size: 20 sections times 12 ticks = 240 frames over at least 10 games, against a bar of at least
    120 frames, at least 20 sections and at least 8 games.

PIN, immediately after selection and before anything else, because the corpus is pruned by age when the
volume passes its quota: for each selected section record the video id, the offset in seconds, the container
byte size, the container SHA-256, and the container facts read back from it -- frame count, frames per
second, width, height and the duration in seconds (contract A9).

FRAME CACHE. The 12 ticks of each section are decoded in one sequential pass per container and written to the
scratch cache as JPEG at quality 95 at NATIVE resolution, under the volume quota rail. The cue, the rater
sheets, the two controls and the eye-check renders all read that ONE archived cache, so every number of this
row recomputes from the committed manifest plus the cache. No claim is made about native pixels versus the
quality-95 re-encode. `frame_key` = SHA-256 over the decoded frame bytes and shape, as G367 defines it.

## 3. ANCHOR LABELS AND THE BLIND PROMPT (verbatim)

Labels: exactly `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `UNKNOWN`. Raters: `terra` and `sol`, run as separate codex lanes on the PC
under separate CODEX_HOME roots, in batches of at most 40 sheets. A sheet is the native frame resized to 960
pixels wide, with no annotation, no overlay, no cue output, no section name and no rating from any other
rater. The sheet file name is the first 12 hex characters of the frame key, so it carries no ordering
information about the section or the tick.

The prompt below is dispatched verbatim, with `<FILES>` replaced by the batch's file names, one per line:

```
You are rating still images from basketball broadcasts. The images are in the directory
docs/evidence/tracking/g378_orientation_cue_2026-09-10/sheets/ relative to the current working directory.
For EACH image file listed at the end, open the image and answer ONE question. Ignoring the broadcast score
banner across the bottom of the picture and any thin scoreboard strip at the very top, which half of the
picture -- the LEFT half or the RIGHT half -- contains more readable venue text? Venue text means lettering
that belongs to the arena rather than to the broadcast overlay: words painted on the playing floor, words on
the courtside advertising boards, words on the scorer's table, and words on the walls, the seats or the
basket stanchion. Answer LEFT if the left half clearly has more, RIGHT if the right half clearly has more,
and UNKNOWN if neither half clearly has more or you cannot tell. Print one line per image and nothing else,
in exactly this form, with no spaces around the comma:
<filename>,<LEFT or RIGHT or UNKNOWN>
The files are:
<FILES>
```

## 4. ADJUDICATION RULE (sealed)

  - Labels are normalised to lower case and written to `ratings.csv` with columns `frame_key,rater,label`.
  - The reference for a frame is the shared label when `terra` and `sol` agree.
  - When they disagree, ONLY the disputed sheets are re-dispatched in a third blind pass under the tag
    `ADJUDICATOR`, with the identical prompt of section 3 and with neither rater's label present anywhere in
    the prompt, the working directory or the sheet. That pass's label is the reference.
  - The join, the agreement rule and the reference rule are `_ratings`, `_reference`, `_wilson` and `_kappa`
    imported from `scripts.platformkit.tracking.g367_cues`; none of them is copied or modified.
  - A frame with no reference is `UNADJUDICATED`. It stays in the decoded denominator, is counted in
    `unknowns.csv`, and is excluded from the per-class correct shares, which is stated wherever those shares
    appear.
  - `UNKNOWN` references are RETAINED. A frame whose reference is UNKNOWN is not a scored class; the cue's
    call on it is reported in the UNKNOWN-reference row and never counted as correct or wrong.
  - Cohen kappa over the two primary raters, three categories, is reported whether or not it clears anything.
    No kappa floor is a bar in this row.

SCORING. The scored set is the frames whose reference is LEFT or RIGHT. For each reference class:
`resolved` = frames of that class where the cue emitted LEFT or RIGHT; `correct` = resolved frames where the
cue equals the reference; the correct share and its Wilson 95 percent interval come from `g367_cues._wilson`.
`resolved / all decoded frames` and the UNKNOWN share are reported over ALL decoded frames, named as the
denominator (contract H1). Every unresolved case is listed in `unknowns.csv` with its reason.

## 5. CONTROLS (sealed, expected 30 of 30 each)

MIRROR CONTROL. Take the frames whose cue call is LEFT or RIGHT, sort them by `frame_key`, and sample 30
EVENLY over that sorted set. Each is flipped horizontally with `cv2.flip(frame, 1)` and re-read by the same
sealed cue. PASS for a frame when the flipped call is the opposite side of the unflipped call.
Expected 30 of 30. If fewer than 30 frames resolve, the control is reported over the frames that exist and
the row is PARTIAL naming this control.

MASKED CONTROL. Sort all decoded frames by `frame_key` and sample 30 EVENLY. In each, the cue's own evidence
band -- rows `[int(_TOP_FRAC * H), int(0.82 * H))`, full width -- is overwritten with the constant value
`(128, 128, 128)` and the frame is re-read by the same sealed cue. PASS for a frame when the call is UNKNOWN.
Expected 30 of 30. Disclosed reconciliation: the spec sentence says "the score bug masked"; the score banner
lies OUTSIDE this cue's band, so masking it could not test anything. The control masks the evidence the cue
actually reads, which is what a no-evidence-yields-UNKNOWN control means (contract B3). The expectation of
30 of 30 is unchanged.

Both control subsets are drawn evenly over a sorted set and never from its head (contract A3, B7).

## 6. ORBIT-CHOICE DIAGNOSTIC (definition, sealed)

Purely diagnostic. It cannot and does not change any G371 status, margin, refusal or bar.

The sealed group is `scripts.platformkit.tracking.g367_symmetry.GROUP` =
(`identity`, `mirror_x`, `rot180`, `mirror_y`), imported, not restated. The four G371 geometries are read
from the landed `matrices_clean.json` keys `G1_SYNTH_QUAD|0.00|000`, `G2_WIDE|0.00|000`,
`G3_TIGHT|0.00|000`, `G4_OFF_AXIS|0.00|000`, using the `winner` matrix and the fixture shape
720 by 1280 recorded in the landed `fixtures.json`.

Anchor probe point `P_ANCHOR = (0.0, 0.0)` in court feet, the template corner, which moves under both
mirror generators. For each geometry and each group element `g`, project `P_ANCHOR` through
`g367_symmetry.compose(winner, g)` and record the image x it lands on and the image half it falls in,
`LEFT` when `x < 640` and `RIGHT` otherwise. Out-of-frame or non-finite projections are recorded as
`OFF_FRAME` and retain nothing.

For each geometry and each of the two cue calls LEFT and RIGHT, `retained` is the list of group elements
whose probe half equals the call, and `n_retained` its length. `unique_after_cue` is true when
`n_retained == 1`. `would_pick_where_g371_refused` is true only when `unique_after_cue` is true AND the
landed G371 cell for that geometry reports more than one distinct symmetry class; the landed value is
1 class on all four, so this field is expected false on all four and the refusal stands untouched. All of
this is written to `orbit_choices.csv` and is never fed back into any G371 artifact.

## 7. BARS (byte-identical to the spec ACCEPTANCE RULE; none may be moved, Q3, B10)

```
  metric        = resolved / all decoded frames; per-class correct share with Wilson 95 pct lower bound; UNKNOWN share; controls; kappa
  before        = G371: orientation UNKNOWN 4/4, 0 accepts; G367: 28 resolved direction labels, quotas unmet
  bar           = >= 120 frames / >= 20 sections / >= 8 games; >= 30 resolved predictions per class AND Wilson lower >= 0.90 per class;
                  controls 30/30 and 30/30; kappa reported; 0 G371 semantics moved (30 perfect predictions alone do not clear 0.90)
  n             = >= 120 frames (SAMPLED, even); 2 raters + adjudication; >= 30 per scored class else PARTIAL
  eye check     = REQUIRED: 30 evenly spaced renders incl. every UNKNOWN and every wrong case
  must not move = G371 refusal/margin semantics, the validator, G362 / G365 bars, the deployed OCR route and weights, every flag
  verdict       = DONE / PARTIAL (name the class or control) / PREMISE FALSE
```

Reading of the bar that is fixed here in advance so it cannot be read favourably afterwards: a class needs
BOTH at least 30 resolved predictions AND a Wilson 95 percent LOWER bound of at least 0.90. Thirty correct
out of thirty gives a lower bound of about 0.884, which does NOT clear 0.90, so quota attainment alone can
never pass this row. Both classes must clear both halves for DONE. Anything short is PARTIAL naming the class
or the control that fell short. Nothing is retried at a lowered bar; a bar found unmeetable is reported at
its limit.

## 8. EYE CHECK (sealed)

Renders are the union of every frame whose cue call is UNKNOWN, every frame where the cue is wrong against a
LEFT or RIGHT reference, and an evenly spaced sample over the scored set sorted by `frame_key`, sized so the
total is at least 30. Each render is the frame at 640 pixels wide with the anchor band border drawn and the
cue call, the reference and the two masses printed on it. The renders are never sampled from the head of any
set. If the union already exceeds 30 the render set grows to cover it and never shrinks below 30.
