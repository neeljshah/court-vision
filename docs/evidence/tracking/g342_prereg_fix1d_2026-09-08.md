# G342 Amendment Preregistration (fix 1d)

Date: 2026-09-08

This amendment is sealed before the finisher runs either synthetic arm. The original
preregistration and fix 1b/fix 1c amendments remain incorporated except where this
document conflicts.

## B8 selection and reporting rule

For every frame, split whole markings into disjoint FIT and VALIDATION subsets as in
fix 1c. Candidate H selection, including the best-of-50 non-true re-fit, uses FIT
markings only. The HEADLINE selector receives `validation_by_frame[i]`: its winner,
bootstrap result, distinctive-group result, confusion label, pairwise diagnostic, and
every reported cost are computed on held-out VALIDATION markings only. FIT markings are
never passed to the selector. `selection_cost_*` is added to decisions.csv; retained
`fit_cost_*` columns are aliases of those validation-side selection costs. Existing
`validation_cost_*` columns remain and are validation-side costs. No existing column is
renamed or removed.

## Archive precision rule

The six-decimal `h00` through `h22` and `cost` fields remain unchanged. archive.csv
adds full `repr(float32)` precision as `h00_repr` through `h22_repr` and `cost_repr`.
observed_polylines.csv additively archives `marking_index`, `x_repr`, and `y_repr` so
the stored held-out markings retain their exact float32 inputs. The new unit test must
recompute ten stored costs from archived full-precision H and held-out inputs to 1e-9.

## Unchanged population and thresholds

The population remains exactly 200 frames per true template per arm, with 40 disjoint
five-frame decisions per template per arm. The unchanged gates are winner cost at most
0.8 times runner-up, at least five frames from at least two shots, at least two
distinctive groups, and the same winner in at least 90 of 100 bootstraps. Arm B remains
independent uniform corner jitter in [-2,2] px and is reported but not gated. Noise,
occlusion, seed, camera ranges, and the best-of-50 approximation remain unchanged.

## Required terminal reporting

The spec verdict line permits only DONE or PARTIAL. If the sealed Arm A bar is not met,
the finisher must put this exact line first in the memo:

`PARTIAL -- BAR NOT MET (true share 0.000000 on 4/4 templates; abstention 320/320)`

The numeric clause is replaced only with the rerun's measured true-share and
abstention values while retaining the exact `PARTIAL -- BAR NOT MET` status prefix.
