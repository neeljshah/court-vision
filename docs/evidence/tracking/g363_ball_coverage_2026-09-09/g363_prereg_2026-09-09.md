## 0. Scope, sign convention, and what this row is not

This is a DETECTION-COVERAGE calibration measurement on broadcast basketball
frames. It is not a downstream tracking, ownership, geometry or forecast claim,
and it carries no monetary interpretation of any kind. Coverage is reported as a
share of sealed reference frames and never as a rate of return.

Sign convention: for coverage, precision, and recall, higher is better and a
displayed delta is CANDIDATE minus BASELINE, so a positive delta means the
candidate is better. For any loss-shaped quantity the convention is BASELINE loss
minus CANDIDATE loss, again positive means the candidate is better. Per-game
paired deltas use the coverage form.

## 1. Source pinning (G361)

A section is identified by its YouTube video id, its section start offset in
seconds, the byte size of the fetched container, and the SHA-256 of that
container, all recorded in `frames.csv` for every reference frame. An NBA game id
alone is NOT a source and makes a section ineligible. The finisher fetches every
section with `/workspace/YTDLP_RECIPE.txt` (rung 270, `--download-sections`,
output template `%(id)s_s%(section_start)d.%(ext)s`, which reproduces the ledger
section id exactly). A re-fetched container may differ from any archived hash by
remux metadata; identity therefore rests on the recorded byte size plus the
frame-level SHA-256 below, never on a joined frame number.

## 2. Eligibility census

`census` reads `data/tracking/track_daemon_ledger.jsonl` (last record per
`game_id`) and `data/tracking/` read-only and writes `census.csv` with, per
section: section id, game (video id), offset, competition (`sport`), status,
rows, decoded_frames, source_resolution, source_fps, directory presence,
eligibility, split, and the selection key. A section is eligible when and only
when (a) its id matches `^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{11}_s[0-9]+$`, so it is source-pinnable,
(b) a tracking output directory exists, and (c) its ledger status is `tracked`.
The census is archived; the reference set is reproducible from it without
re-reading the rotating ledger.

## 3. Split rule -- game-disjointness

Split is a pure function of the game (video id): `development` when
`int(sha256("G363-2026-09-09|" + game)[:8], 16) % 10 < 4`, otherwise `heldout`.
Every section of a game therefore lands in one split. No frame, section or game
crosses the boundary at any point, and the held-out split is touched by exactly
two arm executions (section 7).

## 4. Reference sampling rule -- even, seeded start, never a head slice

Sections are chosen per split by round-robin over games: games are ordered by
their seed digest, sections within a game by theirs, and at most 3 sections are
taken per game, until 40 sections (held-out) or 30 sections (development) are
selected. Frames per section: 16 held-out, 12 development, so the sealed sizes
are 640 held-out and 360 development reference frames.

Within a section of `n` decoded frames drawing `k` frames: `spacing = n // k`,
`start = max(seed_digest(section) % spacing, 2)`, and the indices are
`start + i * spacing` for `i` in `0..k-1`. The start is seeded, the spacing is
fixed, the sample spans the whole section, and a head slice is impossible by
construction. `spacing` below 4 raises rather than sampling. `n` is the container
frame count, falling back to the ledger `decoded_frames` when the container
reports none; a section that decodes short contributes fewer frames and the
shortfall is printed per section and visible in `frames.csv`.

## 5. Frame identity and the decode cache

Each reference frame and its two causal neighbours (t-1, t-2) are decoded ONCE by
a sequential read (never a seek) and cached as PNG under a pod scratch directory.
Frame identity is `sha256(b"G363|" + "<shape>" + b"|" + frame.tobytes())`; that
hash is the `frame_key` and the cache file name. Frames are never joined to any
archived table by frame number. OMP/MKL/OpenBLAS/NumExpr threads and the OpenCV
thread count are pinned to 1 inside the module.

## 6. Label set, blind rater protocol, adjudication

Sheets carry the reference frame rendered at exactly 960 px wide above a
three-frame strip (t-2, t-1, t), JPEG, at most 200 KB, named by the first 12 hex
of the frame key. They carry NO candidate box, NO detector score, NO arm name, NO
coverage value and no burned-in text of any kind.

Two independent raters, `terra` and `sol`, each see only a sheet and this fixed
instruction, and each writes one row of `ratings.csv`
(`frame_key,rater,label,cx,cy,box_w,box_h,reason`):

    Look at the large top image; the strip below is the two frames before it, for
    motion only. Answer for the LARGE image. Label VISIBLE if the game ball is
    visible in it, ABSENT if you are confident no game ball is visible, UNKNOWN if
    you cannot tell. For VISIBLE, give the ball centre cx,cy in pixels of the
    large image, and its box width and height in the same pixels when you can.
    Give a one-line reason. Do not guess a centre for ABSENT or UNKNOWN.

Coordinates are recorded in SHEET pixels of the 960-wide panel; the scorer
converts to native pixels with the `sheet_scale` archived per frame. Where the
two raters agree, that label is the reference. Where they disagree, a third blind
adjudicator (same instruction, same sheet, no rater labels shown) writes a row
with rater `ADJUDICATOR`, and that row is the reference. A frame with neither
agreement nor an adjudication is EXCLUDED from every denominator and counted in
`unadjudicated_frames`; a valid run has that count at zero, and any nonzero value
is printed in the memo as an exclusion (non-tautology: the excluded set is named
and counted, never silently dropped). Cohen kappa over the two primary raters on
the three-label series is reported.

For a VISIBLE reference the centre is the mean of the contributing centres and
the diameter the mean of `max(box_w, box_h)`; when no box was given the reference
diameter is 24 px at 720p.

## 7. Sealed arm list

A0 is the BEFORE measurement, not a development arm: it is the deployed call
exactly (`imgsz=384, conf=0.05, half=True, device=0`, class-0 filter, radius
guard at 50 px), and its coverage on the held-out reference is C0. Fidelity is
asserted, not assumed: for A0 every frame's top-1 output is compared with
`BallDetectTrack._detect_ball_yolo` from the deployed module and the mismatch
count is printed.

Development arms (7 runnable, 1 conditional, at most 8):

    A1  imgsz 640  conf 0.05  single pass
    A2  imgsz 640  conf 0.10  single pass
    A3  imgsz 640  conf 0.25  single pass
    A4  imgsz 960  conf 0.05  single pass
    A5  imgsz 960  conf 0.10  single pass
    A6  imgsz 640  conf 0.05  2x2 tiling, 20 pct overlap, crops taken at native
                              pixel scale, deduped at 24 px between box centres
    A7  the development winner among A1-A6 plus causal 3-tick temporal NMS: the
        two causal neighbours are detected first and marked INFERRED, history is
        reset when the grey 32-bin histogram correlation between t-1 and t is
        below 0.5, and an observed box farther than 96 px at 720p from the linear
        extrapolation 2*c(t-1) - c(t-2) is DROPPED. History rows are archived
        with rank -1 and are never a true positive.
    A8  a small fine-tune (frozen backbone, last layers, at most 10 epochs) runs
        ONLY if at least 500 audited observed boxes from at least 5 development
        games exist. Otherwise it is SKIPPED and reported as a LIMIT.

Winner rule, applied to development results only: among A1-A7, the arm with the
highest coverage subject to Wilson lower precision at least 0.90 and
`FP / N_absent` at most 0.01; if no arm satisfies both constraints, the winner is
the arm with the highest Wilson lower precision bound and the held-out result is
reported against the bar as NOT VALIDATED. Ties resolve by smaller imgsz, then
higher conf, then arm id ascending.

The held-out split receives exactly two executions: A0 and the single selected
candidate. No other arm ever touches it.

## 8. Matching rule

Only rank-0 OBSERVED predictions are scored. Matching is one-to-one and greedy in
ascending distance: a prediction matches a reference when the centre distance,
normalised to 720p by `720 / native_height`, is at most
`max(3 px, reference_diameter_720p / 2)`. Each reference absorbs at most one
prediction and each prediction matches at most one reference. Every unmatched
prediction and every duplicate is a FALSE POSITIVE. On an ABSENT frame and on an
UNKNOWN frame there is no reference, so every prediction there is a FALSE
POSITIVE. UNKNOWN frames stay in the full-frame denominator and their share is
printed.

## 9. Metric definitions

    N            = sealed reference frames of the split with an adjudicated label
                   (VISIBLE + ABSENT + UNKNOWN); never the count of frames that
                   happen to carry a detection (contract H1)
    C            = TP / N
    precision    = TP / (TP + FP), with the two-sided Wilson 95 pct interval
    FP / N_absent  = TOTAL false positives of the arm divided by N_absent (the
                   spec's literal form, which is the bar); the restricted form,
                   false positives occurring ON absent frames per absent frame, is
                   reported beside it and is not the bar
    recall       = TP / N_visible
    abstention   = frames with zero scored predictions / N
    paired delta = per game, C(arm, game) - C(A0, game); mean, min, max and the
                   count of improved games are reported (contract Q9: the per-game
                   series is archived in `frame_scores.csv`, one row per frame per
                   arm, so every interval is recomputable from the artifacts alone)

## 10. Bars and verdict ladder -- byte-identical to the spec, never moved

DONE requires, on the HELD-OUT reference, all three of:

    C1 >= 3 x C0
    Wilson lower bound of precision >= 0.90
    FP / N_absent <= 0.01

and at least 150 VISIBLE and at least 150 ABSENT references. Otherwise the
verdict is NOT VALIDATED (bars met on none or some) or PARTIAL (reference
composition short). A bar found unmeetable is reported CLOSED AT LIMIT and is
never lowered.

## 11. LIMIT and PREMISE FALSE conditions

    C0 >= 0.50                       -> PREMISE FALSE: stop, memo, commit, report
    C0 == 0                          -> LIMIT: a threefold increase is undefined
    N_visible < 150 or N_absent < 150 -> PARTIAL
    fewer than 500 audited boxes      -> A8 skipped, reported as a LIMIT on A8
    a section that cannot be fetched or decoded -> reported ABSENT-SECTION and
      excluded, with the count named in the memo

The G357 survival figure (`docs/evidence/tracking/g357_ball_ownership_px_attempt2_2026-09-08.md`,
`any_ball_row` at 272 per mille of pooled frames, detected_ball at 192 per mille)
is printed beside C0 for context; it is a different denominator on a different
corpus and is NOT the before-value of this row.

## 12. Finisher commands, in order

Run from `/workspace/wt/a7` on the pod. `PYTHONPATH` is set explicitly because an
editable install otherwise resolves `scripts/` to the main checkout.

    EV=docs/evidence/tracking/g363_ball_coverage_2026-09-09
    S=/workspace/g363_scratch
    export PYTHONPATH=/workspace/wt/a7

    # 1. census (read-only; archives eligibility)
    python3 -m scripts.platformkit.tracking.g363_ball_coverage census \
      --ledger /workspace/data/tracking/track_daemon_ledger.jsonl \
      --tracking-root /workspace/data/tracking --out $EV/census.csv

    # 2. pin + fetch every selected section into $S/sections with the pod recipe
    #    (yt-dlp -f 270 --download-sections "*<OFF>-<OFF+130>"
    #     -o "$S/sections/%(id)s_s%(section_start)d.%(ext)s"), then

    # 3. sample: decode each reference frame and its two causal neighbours once
    python3 -m scripts.platformkit.tracking.g363_ball_coverage sample \
      --census $EV/census.csv --sections-dir $S/sections --cache $S/cache \
      --out $EV/frames.csv

    # 4. blind sheets
    python3 -m scripts.platformkit.tracking.g363_ball_coverage sheets \
      --frames $EV/frames.csv --cache $S/cache --out $S/sheets

    # 5. raters terra and sol, then the blind adjudicator -> $EV/ratings.csv

    # 6. premise: A0 on the held-out reference -> C0, then the premise gate
    python3 -m scripts.platformkit.tracking.g363_ball_coverage arms --arm A0 \
      --frames $EV/frames.csv --cache $S/cache --split heldout \
      --deploy-root /workspace/deploy/nba-ai-system --out $EV/predictions.csv

    # 7. development, ONE AT A TIME, A0 first then A1..A6, then A7 --inherit <winner>
    python3 -m scripts.platformkit.tracking.g363_ball_coverage arms --arm A1 \
      --frames $EV/frames.csv --cache $S/cache --split development \
      --deploy-root /workspace/deploy/nba-ai-system --out $EV/predictions.csv

    # 8. freeze the winner, then the single held-out candidate run
    python3 -m scripts.platformkit.tracking.g363_ball_coverage arms --arm A7 \
      --inherit <winner> --frames $EV/frames.csv --cache $S/cache --split heldout \
      --deploy-root /workspace/deploy/nba-ai-system --out $EV/predictions.csv

    # 9. score everything at once
    python3 -m scripts.platformkit.tracking.g363_ball_coverage score \
      --frames $EV/frames.csv --ratings $EV/ratings.csv \
      --predictions $EV/predictions.csv --frame-scores $EV/frame_scores.csv \
      --arms-out $EV/arms.csv --out $EV/summary.json

    # 10. per-file test only
    python3 -m pytest tests/platformkit/test_g363_ball_coverage.py -q -p no:cacheprovider

GPU protocol: arms run STRICTLY SEQUENTIALLY on the single RTX 3090. Each run
reads free VRAM first and refuses below 4000 MiB; no process is ever killed or
restarted; GPU minutes and decoded fps are printed per arm and quoted in the memo.
Sections are deleted from scratch after `sample`; the PNG cache is kept until
scoring finishes and is then deleted. Only `census.csv`, `frames.csv`,
`ratings.csv`, `predictions.csv`, `frame_scores.csv`, `arms.csv`, `summary.json`
and the 30 evenly spaced eye-check sheets are committed under `docs/evidence/`;
the full sheet set stays in scratch and is identified by the SHA-256 recorded in
`sheets_manifest.csv`.
