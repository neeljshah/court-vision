## Frozen calibration protocol

The independent raters are `terra` and `sol`. Each view is one native
640x540 tile with its explicit integer offset in a 1920x1080 context. The
finisher records an image-open receipt and a tile-to-native conversion receipt
for every dispatched observation. It does not use a contact sheet.

Create 30 PRACTICE and 30 distinct QUALIFICATION controls on the retained
G388 context identities using seed 392. Each control archives pixels, finite
centreline truth, one of six tile positions, and a 0/30/60 degree cycle. No
pixel/truth tuple occurs in both sets. Practice feedback permits one recorded
rater-side instruction or display correction. Freeze the correction receipt,
mapping, and instructions before qualification begins.

Qualification is blind and receives no feedback. Each rater supplies three
independent points: the first and second span at least 60 native pixels, the
third is interior, every point projects within the finite band, and every
point has perpendicular distance at most 3 native pixels. Missing output is a
failure. Each rater and their joint contexts must pass at least 27 of 30.
There is no substitute, retry, fitted offset, or blanket displacement.

If either rater fails, real dispatch is blocked and the verdict is `PARTIAL --
PROTOCOL NOT QUALIFIED`. If both raters qualify, use G388's sealed even 30 of
49 real contexts. Raters mark at most two fragments in fixed tile order with
the allowed family values in the specification, retaining ABSENT, UNKNOWN,
and unmatched fragments. Pair by tile/family and stable smallest symmetric
line distance. The third point, pair tolerance, and nine-point native-paint
audit remain exactly 3 px, 6 px, and 8/9 at 3 px respectively.

Arithmetic is repeated in separate processes. This preparation has no
measured delta. If a later comparison records one, its sign convention is
improvement = baseline loss minus candidate loss; positive means candidate
better.
