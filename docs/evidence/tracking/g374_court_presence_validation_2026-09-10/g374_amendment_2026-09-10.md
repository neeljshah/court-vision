## A1 -- the identity field the binding must read

The sealed prereg says the binding "passes only when the identity and model SHA-256
agree". The landed `model_identity.json` carries the FEATURE EXTRACTOR identity
only: `device`, `embedding_dim`, `extractor`, `weights_path`, `weights_sha256`. It
carries no head digest, so `g374_premise._identity_sha256` cannot resolve one and
the binding raises before printing the census.

The frozen head digest is `sha256(model.json)`. `validation_predictions.csv` binds
every one of its 360 rows to that digest in its `model_sha256` column. The binding
is therefore amended to check, in place of the unresolvable identity field:

1. `sha256(model.json)` equals the single distinct `model_sha256` value present in
   `validation_predictions.csv`;
2. `model_identity.json.weights_sha256` equals `model.json.feature_extractor_sha256`,
   which pins the frozen extractor the head was fitted on.

Threshold exactly 0.30, whole-pool census 360 (224 COURT, 122 NON_COURT, 14
ABSTAIN), and empty development/validation game overlap are unchanged and still
binding. A failure of any of the four is still PREMISE FALSE.

## A2 -- the pinned sources were absent and are restored, not substituted

The sealed prereg names five frozen inputs and assumes the pinned video sources are
readable. At finisher time `/workspace/g364_scratch/val/` was empty: all 30 pinned
validation sources named in `sources_validation.csv` were absent from the pod, and
no copy existed anywhere under `/workspace`. Blind sheets cannot be built without
them. This is a missing phase rule, not a falsified premise: the head, the
threshold, the census and the split are all intact.

The sources are RESTORED, never substituted. `g374_recover.py` re-fetches each
pinned section with the recorded `g364_census.fetch` recipe into the pinned path and
verifies the result byte-for-byte:

- a source whose re-fetched SHA-256 equals its `sources_validation.csv` digest is
  `restored` and is used exactly as pinned;
- a source that does not reproduce its digest is `mismatch`, a source that cannot be
  fetched is `fetch_failed`, and in BOTH cases every frame key belonging to that
  section is removed from the draw before the draw, listed in `excluded.csv` with
  reason `source_not_reproducible`, and named in the memo;
- the per-source ledger `sources_restored.csv` (`section_id, source_path,
  expected_sha256, observed_sha256, expected_bytes, observed_bytes, status`) is
  archived beside the other evidence and is part of the landing.

No re-encode, no re-probe substitution, no resolution change and no alternative
source is permitted. A restored file is used only on a byte-identical digest.

## A3 -- what the exclusion does to the verdict

If any section is excluded under A2 the sealed availability changes, so the quota is
run on the reduced pool by the SAME sealed rule (all ABSTAIN frames, then COURT and
NON_COURT filled evenly at fixed seeded spacing in proportion to availability, each
at least 60, total exactly 300). If the reduced pool cannot supply 300 keys or
cannot hold either 60-frame floor, the row reports PARTIAL and names the unmet bar
with the counts; the bars themselves are never lowered and the draw is never
re-seeded to reach them.

Nothing else changes. The frozen head, the threshold 0.30, the four-class blind
rating, the two raters plus blind adjudication, the one-shot scoring, the
`confusion.csv` and prediction-manifest field lists, the Wilson bounds, the
precision 0.90 and recall 0.80 bars, and the B8 NOT-evidence labelling of the
development numbers are all as sealed. No refit, no threshold move, no flag, no
register edit, no `data/registry/` write.
