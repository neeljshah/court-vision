# G350 preregistration

Date: 2026-09-08
Spec: docs/evidence/tracking/specs/G350_spec.md
Verifier: docs/evidence/tracking/VERIFIER_CONTRACT.md (A/B; Q1-Q4 self-check)

## Scope and inputs

This is a blinded validation of the landed G341 view-class cue, not a calibration or
geometric-validity claim. Inputs are read one video at a time from the staged corpus or
the pod's read-only corpus link. Each input record names its full resolved a14 path,
SHA-256, byte size, native width, and native height. The fixed router is
scripts/platformkit/tracking/shot_router.py at 320x180 and 5 fps; its source hash is
recorded in the final memo. No router threshold, flag, `src/`, or `data/` path is edited.

## Sealed selection

Candidate units are G341 `shot_id` runs from a deterministic route of the declared input
inventory. A unit key is `sha256(video bytes):shot_id`; its representative is the median
kept frame and its strip comprises the first, median, and final kept frames. The predicted
class is the most frequent frame class in the run; ties resolve in this fixed order:
WIDE, CLOSEUP, CROWD, UNKNOWN. Selection is a SHA-256 sort on
`G350-2026-09-08|unit_key`, taking the first 20 WIDE, first 20 CLOSEUP, and first 20 of
the combined CROWD/UNKNOWN group while preserving at least six distinct video hashes. If
any required group or the six-game condition is unavailable, write PARTIAL with the full
candidate counts; do not substitute, rebalance after ratings, or score a smaller set.

## Blind rating and reference

Each selected sheet contains only the unit id, representative frame, and three-frame strip;
it contains no router class, prediction, decision, or aggregate. Terra and Sol independently
assign exactly one of USABLE_WIDE, CLOSEUP, CROWD_GRAPHICS, UNKNOWN and a one-line reason.
The two labels are retained. Equal labels form the reference; unequal labels go unchanged
to a third blinded adjudicator, whose single label is the reference. Cohen kappa is reported
between the two original raters, with the four labels in this exact order. Model-rater
agreement is a proxy for label quality, not human annotation.

## Scoring and bar

All 60 selected units must be rated by both primary raters. Router WIDE is the accepted
class; reference USABLE_WIDE is the positive class. Report the complete confusion counts,
Wilson 95 percent intervals for precision(WIDE) and recall(USABLE_WIDE), abstention share
(router UNKNOWN / 60), per-game counts, and the G341 propagation cut check for every rated
unit. The bar is byte-identical to the spec: precision(WIDE) >= 0.95 and
recall(USABLE_WIDE) >= 0.80 on the 60 shots. If either condition fails, verdict is NOT
VALIDATED and downstream use remains UNKNOWN-safe. Delta convention for any displayed
delta is baseline loss minus candidate loss; positive means candidate better. No such
paired-loss comparison is part of this static confusion audit, so walk-forward/CPCV,
purging, embargo, evaluator states, and differential losses are not applicable.

## Fixed tests

The focused test uses an exhaustive hand-pinned 12-shot construct with known four-class
confusion counts and Cohen kappa, and asserts that generated sheets contain no router-label
strings. It is not a corpus score.
