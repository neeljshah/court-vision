# G397 preregistration: mixed-source producer census

Status: PREPARE-ONLY. This protocol is sealed before any census, source probe,
capture, decode, reconstruction, arithmetic, render, eye review, or comparison.
Claude finisher alone performs the measurement after this file is committed.

## Scope and premise binding

The binding before-condition is a whole-current-set census of the actual daemon
ledger beneath the daemon executable's resolved current working directory. It
must resolve the ledger path, snapshot it once, record its digest, and preserve
the fields `game_id`, `status`, `rows`, `decoded_frames`, `evaluated_frames`,
`stride`, `source_height`, `source_fps`, and `source_duration`. It must also
prove both actual format groups and ORIGINAL route identity across the census.
The source-policy admission time alone is not evidence of the premise.

If an existing completed format-effect census is found, the finisher records
PREMISE FALSIFIED only after rerunning that exact binding condition and quoting
its output. If fewer than 30 unique sections of either defined kind exist, or
source identities are not bound, the finisher reports PARTIAL with the complete
distribution and does not select a smaller convenient set.

## Frozen definitions and draw

Freeze one UTC census time before opening selected objects. Retain every
attempt/source identity available across the policy boundary and separately
record feeder DROP/probe records, selector and probe file digests, and policy
timestamps. Probe each candidate object for raw width, height, average and
r-frame-rate rationals, time base, duration, and decoded PTS.

Classify `1080p30` only when width=1920, height=1080, and measured fps is in
[29,31]. Classify `720p60` only when width=1280, height=720, and measured fps
is in [59,61]. Retain OTHER and UNKNOWN as their own statuses. Never infer a
format from an itag. Within each kind sort unique sections by
competition/video/source-start/section/digest, then choose indices
floor(j*(N-1)/29+0.5), j=0..29. The draw has no replacement: every selected
section retains all attempts, including failures and repeats.

For the primary output select the frozen earliest terminal ORIGINAL attempt per
section. An absent or nonterminal attempt is represented explicitly as missing.
The retained table bundle is `tracking_data.csv`, `ball_tracking.csv`,
`evaluated_frame_count.json`, `harness_verdict.json`, launch argv, and the full
matching ledger records. Copies are one-pass receiver readbacks under the G386
contract; cache-wide duplication is forbidden.

## Schedule, denominators, and descriptive calculation

Reconstruct evaluated ticks only from `ball_tracking.csv` rows with integral
`frame` and `live==1`, using G376. `live==0` frames are suspended. Missing,
cleared, unreadable, or inconsistent schedule tables make the evaluated
denominator UNKNOWN; player-emitting frames and a declared stride grid are not
substitutes. Check producer decoded-frame counts against retained source decode
under the recorded cap and name source-decode and producer-decode separately.

For each section retain raw rows, unique player/tick rows, decoded frames,
declared/evaluated/emitting ticks, suspended ticks, and evaluated ticks with
zero player rows. Rows per decoded frame and rows per evaluated tick retain
their own denominators. Held share is byte-equal x/y pairs for a shared player
on consecutive evaluated ticks divided by all such shared-player pairs. Report
no-shared-player adjacent pairs and no-output evaluated ticks separately; never
bridge a missing evaluated tick.

The finisher records cap, stride, elapsed source PTS, cap/fps implied duration,
decoder version, and fps handling. It keeps native fps with no resampling and
separates equal-PTS-duration summaries from equal-frame-cap summaries. It may
report only descriptive 720p60-minus-1080p30 differences, missingness, and
competition/video composition; no pooled producer-quality or resolution-only
attribution is permitted.

## Provenance, repeats, and artifacts

Audit `source_height` and `source_fps` against probes on every selected ledger
record, including failure records; absent values remain UNKNOWN. Export an
additive enriched ledger copy that joins every selected attempt once, preserves
original fields, and records exact joins and digests. Runtime repair, active
schema wiring, flags, and deployment remain out of scope.

The finisher recomputes canonical tables twice from snapshots and records both
digests. It constructs an evenly ordered native/metadata card index with one
native interior frame and output/timeline overlay per selected section. Planned
artifacts are `policy_receipt.json`, `census.csv`, `draw.csv`,
`source_probes.csv`, `source_receipts.csv`, `ledger_snapshot.jsonl`,
`enriched_ledger.jsonl`, `per_section.csv`, `evaluated_ticks.csv`,
`held_pairs.csv`, `snapshots/`, `eye_index.csv`, `summary.json`, `repeats.json`,
and common receipts. Every absent or failed state is retained.

## Completion limits

Complete format accounting requires 30 sections of each kind, all attempts and
source identities accounted, and measured height/fps for each classified source.
Producer descriptive comparison additionally requires all 60 retained records,
at least 30 complete evaluated schedules per kind, zero silent omission, and
exactly reproducible rates. Enriched provenance requires every selected attempt
to join once without invented values. A missing denominator remains UNKNOWN.

Sign convention for any future loss delta: improvement equals baseline loss
minus candidate loss; positive means the candidate is better. This protocol
creates no loss delta and no measurement result.
