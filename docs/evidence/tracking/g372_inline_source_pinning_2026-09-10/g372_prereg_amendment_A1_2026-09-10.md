A1 CODE IDENTITY. The two exact before-condition commands of `docs/evidence/tracking/specs/G372_spec.md` run verbatim under `/workspace/deploy/nba-ai-system` and their complete stdout is archived. A2 SURVIVAL AND ATTRIBUTION CENSUS. Population: every row of `/workspace/data/tracking/track_daemon_ledger.jsonl` from row 856 to the end of an immutable lane snapshot taken before any counting; row 856 is the first row of this pod's 2026-09-10 fresh era and the boundary is fixed by the snapshot, never by a later re-read. Unit: unique `game_id`; ledger rows are counted separately and never used as the section denominator. Claim time of a row is `finished_at - seconds`. Byte location of a section is BRIDGE if `<sport>__<game_id>.mp4` is in `footage_bridge`, CORPUS if in `footage_corpus`, QUARANTINE if in `footage_quarantine`, else GONE; `.failed` renames and `source_variants` names are probed before a section is called GONE. RE-TRACKED means the section has more than one ledger row in the snapshot, or its `data/tracking/<game_id>` directory mtime is newer than its first row's `finished_at`. DUP GATE split: a section is BEFORE if its first claim time is earlier than 2026-09-10T17:49:00Z and AFTER otherwise; the two survival shares are reported side by side with their own denominators and neither is reported alone. A stratum with fewer than 30 sections is labelled descriptive, never a scored survival claim.

A3 SCRATCH SIDECAR EXERCISE. Sources are collected into `/workspace/wt/a9/scratch_g372/`, never into `/workspace/data` and never into the deploy tree, by polling `footage_bridge` and `footage_corpus` every 120 s for at most 120 min, copying each not-yet-collected `<sport>__<game_id>.mp4` (per-file cap 4 GiB, total cap 60 GiB) and stopping at 40 distinct `game_id` or at the window end, whichever comes first. Every attempted collection is retained in the manifest with its outcome, including copies that fail or vanish mid-read. The sidecar and the independent check both read the SCRATCH bytes, so the comparison is well defined even when the live source is moved or removed afterwards. The independent check imports only low-level helpers of `g369_prospective_identity` (`_file_record`, `_probe`, `_manifest`) and never `cmd_pin` or `cmd_exact_align`. Join key: `game_id` plus the scratch source SHA-256; a row that cannot be joined on both is reported as a failure, never dropped. Bars, unchanged from the spec: coverage 1.000 over collected sections, independent agreement 100 pct, zero omitted selected rows. Fewer than 30 collected sections or fewer than 10 distinct videos makes the sampled clauses NOT VALIDATED for phase A; the window is not extended after seeing the agreement result and a second window is not substituted.

A4 CONTROLS, CONSTRUCT and separately enumerated. Byte-flip: flipping one byte of a scratch copy changes both the sidecar digest and the independent digest. Identical-read: two independent reads of the same scratch bytes agree. Deterministic-write: two sidecar writes over the same fixed payload are byte-identical. Each control names its input kind and is never counted toward the sampled n.
