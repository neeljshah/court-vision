A2.2 THE ATTEMPT SET. Every unique `game_id` in the window is exactly ONE claim attempt, whether or not its bytes exist anywhere at collection time. Attempts are ordered ascending by `(first_claim_utc, game_id)`, where `first_claim_utc = finished_at - seconds` of the section's earliest row, and carry index `i = 0 .. n-1` in that order. `n` is the count of unique `game_id` in the window; ledger rows are never the denominator.

A2.3 THE SAMPLE, even by construction. If `n <= 40` the sample is ALL `n` attempts. Otherwise `k = floor(n / 40)` and the sample is every k-th index beginning at `s = int(snapshot_sha256[0:8], 16) mod k`, i.e. indices `s, s+k, s+2k, ...` while `< n`. The start is derived from a digest that predates this amendment and is therefore fixed, not chosen. `n`, `k`, `s`, every sampled index and its `game_id` are archived to `sampled_indices.csv`. A head slice, a re-draw, a second seed, a different start and any post-hoc extension of the sample are all forbidden.

A2.4 FAILURES ARE RETAINED, never dropped. Each sampled attempt is collected by probing `<sport>__<game_id>.mp4`, its `.failed` rename and its recorded `source_variants` names under `footage_bridge`, `footage_corpus` and `footage_quarantine`, and copying the bytes to `/workspace/wt/a9/scratch_g372/sources/`. Every sampled attempt gets exactly one row in `collect_manifest.csv` with exactly one status: `OK`, `BYTES_GONE` (no file under any location), `SKIP_OVER_CAP` (over the 4 GiB per-file cap), or `ERROR_<type>` (open, stat or copy failure). Bytes collected before this seal are discarded and not reused. The manifest -- never a directory listing, never a sorted glob -- is the sole input of the exercise step.

A2.5 DENOMINATORS. `coverage = valid immutable sidecars committed / ALL sampled attempts`. `agreement = joined rows whose sidecar SHA-256 and byte size equal the independent G369 read / ALL sampled attempts`. Every non-`OK` attempt is LEFT-JOINED into `join.csv` carrying its manifest status and reason, so the failure classes sit in the same table as the successes and are counted in both denominators. A second, clearly labelled conditional figure (agreement over joined rows only) may be printed beside them, never instead of them.

A2.6 BARS, unchanged and not moved. Coverage 1.000; independent agreement 100 pct; `>= 30` sampled sections; `>= 10` distinct videos (spec ACCEPTANCE RULE, byte-identical). A phase-A coverage or agreement below its bar is reported as a NAMED FAILING CLAUSE of the PARTIAL verdict -- never rounded away, never cured by re-collecting, never restated as a pass. Fewer than 30 sampled attempts or fewer than 10 distinct videos makes the affected clause NOT VALIDATED for phase A. The post-hoc character of this exercise is the expected reason for a low coverage number: A1's census already measured that the pod volume guard prunes a section's bytes a few minutes after tracking finishes, which is exactly the condition claim-time binding exists to remove. A low phase-A coverage number is therefore a measurement of that pruning, NOT evidence for or against the overlay, and it licenses no statement about phase-B claim-time coverage in either direction.
