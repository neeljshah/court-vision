A3.2 THE SMOKE GATE, before any restart. The patched module is byte-compiled, imported with `PYTHONPATH=/workspace/deploy/nba-ai-system`, and its claim path is exercised once, out of process, on ONE scratch copy of a staged source, writing only under `/workspace/wt/a9/scratch_g372/`. The smoke passes only if a sidecar is committed carrying all eleven fields of `SIDE_FIELDS` and a reservation row is journaled. A smoke failure REVERTS the deploy tree from the backup, the memo reads PHASE B NOT DEPLOYED with the failure named, and no restart happens.

A3.3 THE RESTART, exactly once. `T_RESTART` is the UTC second at which SIGTERM is sent to the pid read from `/workspace/track_daemon.pid`. The daemon is relaunched only by `bash /workspace/start_daemon.sh 8`. In-flight children at SIGTERM are recorded by name and their fate (finished, reaped, re-claimed) is reported. WATCHDOG: if within 20 minutes of relaunch the new daemon has not both claimed a staged source and written one completion ledger row, or its log carries a traceback naming the overlay, the deploy tree is REVERTED from the backup, the original daemon is restarted, and the verdict is PHASE B REVERTED with the log excerpt. A second restart for any other reason is forbidden.

A3.4 THE BEFORE WINDOW, fixed by the restart and not chosen. An immutable ledger snapshot is taken at `T_RESTART`, before the relaunch, and its SHA-256 is archived. BEFORE = every completed row of that snapshot with `finished_at` in `[T_RESTART - 3600, T_RESTART)`. Per-row seconds per clip is `finished_at - (finished_at - seconds)`, i.e. the ledger `seconds` field, which is claim-to-finish. Reported: n rows, n unique `game_id`, median and quartiles of seconds per clip, clips per hour, arrivals (distinct `game_id` first claimed in the window), mean active load, and the per-sport split. A window is never re-cut and never extended.

A3.5 THE AFTER WINDOW, >= 60 minutes. `T_FIRST_CLAIM` is the earliest `claim_utc` in the deployed claim journal `data/tracking/source_identity/claim_journal.jsonl` at or after `T_RESTART`. AFTER = every completed row with `finished_at` in `[T_FIRST_CLAIM, T_FIRST_CLAIM + 3600)`, cut from a snapshot taken no earlier than `T_FIRST_CLAIM + 3600`, SHA-256 archived. A row whose claim time precedes `T_RESTART` is RETAINED and flagged STRADDLE; the median is printed over ALL rows and over non-straddle rows, both, never one alone. Jobs still running at the cut are CENSORED, counted and named, never dropped. A second window is not substituted for an inconvenient first one.

A3.6 COVERAGE, denominator first. Denominator = every unique `game_id` carrying a RESERVED row in the claim journal with `claim_utc` inside the AFTER window. Numerator = those `game_id` whose sidecar exists, parses, and carries all eleven `SIDE_FIELDS` keys. TERMINAL_ERROR reservations stay in the denominator and are named. Bar: coverage 1.000 over >= 30 sections and >= 10 distinct videos; fewer than 30 reservations or fewer than 10 distinct videos makes the clause NOT VALIDATED, never a pass.

A3.7 THE INDEPENDENT PIN. The finisher watches `data/footage_bridge` and, within 60 s of each observed claim, copies the staged source to scratch and reads it with the G369 helpers only -- `g369_prospective_identity._file_record`, `._probe`, `._manifest` -- never `cmd_pin` and never `cmd_exact_align`. Join key: `game_id` plus source SHA-256. Agreement = joined rows whose sidecar SHA-256 and byte size equal the independent read, over ALL attempted pins; a pin that could not be taken is retained with its status and never dropped. Bar: 100 pct on >= 30 pins over >= 10 distinct videos; fewer makes the clause NOT VALIDATED. Caveat carried from phase A: both sides stream SHA-256, so agreement proves the two code paths bind the same bytes, not that the digest is correct against a third implementation.

A3.8 CONTROLS on LIVE sidecars (CONSTRUCT, enumerated separately, never counted toward n): identical-read, two independent reads of one committed sidecar are byte-identical; deterministic-write, two writes of one fixed payload are byte-identical. Each names its input.

A3.9 SCHEMA AND LEDGER FIELDS. The completion-ledger key set of the BEFORE rows and of the AFTER rows is computed and diffed. The only permitted difference is the ADDITIVE key `source_identity_path`; any removed, renamed or type-changed key is a failing clause. One producer table key set is diffed before versus after and MUST be empty.

A3.10 THE PAIRED THROUGHPUT REPLAY. It begins only after the AFTER window closes, so it never perturbs the live measurement. ELIGIBLE SET: every `*.mp4` in `/workspace/data/footage_corpus` in one listing taken at replay start, of size <= 200 MB, whose `game_id` carries a `tracked` ledger row. If the eligible n <= 34 all are used; otherwise `k = floor(n / 34)` and every k-th entry ordered by `(mtime, name)` is taken, starting at index `int(sha256(listing)[0:8], 16) mod k` -- the same even-sampling construction A2.3 sealed, never a head slice. ARMS, on one source each, same thread caps, identical `build_command` arguments including `--frames 3000`: CONTROL = `scripts/run_clip.py` alone; TREATMENT = the patched claim path (reservation, journal append, streamed digest, bounded probe) followed by one full `--interval` deferral of 20 s, which is the worst-case tick the patched loop imposes before dispatch, followed by the identical `run_clip`. Both arms of one pair run back to back in the same worker; at most 6 pair-workers run concurrently so contention is symmetric across arms. STOPPING: a fixed budget of 100 minutes of replay wall time, sealed here and not extended. METRIC: after/before median seconds per clip over completed pairs; bar <= 1.10, never slower. Fewer than 30 completed pairs makes the throughput clause NOT VALIDATED, naming the achieved n; unchanged clips per hour in the window comparison is never a pass on its own.

A3.11 BARS NOT MOVED. Every bar above is byte-identical to the spec ACCEPTANCE RULE: coverage 1.000, >= 30 sections, >= 10 distinct videos, agreement 100 pct, after/before <= 1.10, empty producer schema diff. A bar found unmeetable is reported NOT VALIDATED or CLOSED AT LIMIT, never lowered.
