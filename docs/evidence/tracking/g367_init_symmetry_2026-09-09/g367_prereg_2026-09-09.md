## 0. Scope, sign convention, and what this row is not
A GEOMETRY-RECOVERY calibration measurement on synthetic court fixtures, plus a small labelled
BROADCAST measurement of one orientation cue. Not a tracking, ownership, downstream or forecast
claim, and no monetary interpretation. Every pixel quantity here is a distance between two
homographies' images of the same sealed template, or a count over synthetic supports.

Sign convention. A GAP is a loss in image pixels: lower is better, and an improvement is BASELINE
minus CANDIDATE, so positive means the candidate is better. The section 4 OBJECTIVE score is a gain
in [0, 1] and a margin is TOP minus RUNNER-UP. PRECISION in section 8 is a gain.

## 1. Template coordinate frame and the sealed court symmetry group
The template frame is `scripts/platformkit/tracking/g334_court_template.py`, imported unchanged:
`x` is the court WIDTH in feet, 0 to 50; `y` is the court LENGTH in feet, 0 to 94; the fitted
template is the NEAR HALF, so its 398 points occupy y in [0, 47]. `TEMPLATE_POINTS` and
`HALF_COURT_QUAD` are read from that module and never redefined here.

The sealed group is four 3x3 transforms OF THE TEMPLATE FRAME, applied to court feet in homogeneous
coordinates, in this fixed order (ties in every argmin below resolve to the earliest):

    identity  I  = [[ 1, 0,  0], [0,  1,  0], [0, 0, 1]]
    mirror_x  X  = [[-1, 0, 50], [0,  1,  0], [0, 0, 1]]    x -> 50 - x
    rot180    R  = [[-1, 0, 50], [0, -1, 94], [0, 0, 1]]    180 deg about the court centre (25, 47)
    mirror_y  Y  = [[ 1, 0,  0], [0, -1, 94], [0, 0, 1]]    y -> 94 - y, the end swap

Closure is proved in the test, not asserted here: X X = I, R R = I, Y Y = I, X R = R X = Y,
X Y = Y X = R, R Y = Y R = X, so it is the Klein four-group; the identity has order 1 and each of X,
R and Y is an involution. The test checks all sixteen products against a written table.

Structural note, arithmetic and not a measurement: X maps the near half onto itself, while R and Y
map y in [0, 47] onto y in [47, 94] and cannot stand for the same near-half ink. Both are scored
anyway, so the artifact shows what they attain instead of assuming it. Including them can only LOWER
a modulo gap, so they cannot flatter the section 6 bar, and the finisher prints the measured
per-element template invariance in feet (`summary.json.premise.template_invariance_ft`) beside the
table. G365 measured the X invariance at 0.509294 ft, half the template 1 ft sampling step.

## 2. recovery_modulo_symmetry -- the sealed definition
For a truth homography T (court feet -> image pixels) and a candidate H:

    labelled gap   g(T, H)  = reprojection_median(T, H, TEMPLATE_POINTS)
                            = median over the 398 template points p of
                              || project(T, p) - project(H, p) ||, in image pixels
    modulo gap     g*(T, H) = min over S in (I, X, R, Y) of g(T S, H)
    attaining element       = the argmin, ties by the sealed order above

`reprojection_median` is imported from `g362_synth.py` unchanged (B8: it never inspects residuals
against the lines a fit consumed). Because I is in the group, g* <= g always, and BOTH numbers
appear in every table with the attaining element. No fixture label is changed by this row. The
alternative form g(T, H S) coincides with g(T S, H) exactly when H is an exact symmetry image and
approximately otherwise, to the extent the template point SET is S-invariant; form ONE is the sealed
definition, and the finisher prints form TWO beside it for X as a diagnostic, never as a bar.

## 4. INITIALISATION SEARCH -- sealed enumeration, scoring, refinement and ambiguity
Per geometry, on the SAME painted raster G362 and G365 used (`g362_synth.render_court(T)`, then
`g362_fit_validate.to_base_height`):

    1. strokes = g362_strokes.extract_strokes(frame, ARM_A); (fit, val) = partition(strokes). The
       search sees the FIT strokes' segments and nothing else (B8); the VALIDATION strokes are never
       given to any fit or any refinement in this row.
    2. field = g334_court_template.distance_transform(frame.shape, fit segments).
    3. families = split_families; groups = family_groups, truncated to the sealed K = 7 longest
       groups per family. K is ARM_A.top_k unchanged -- named here, never moved (B10, Q3).
    4. hypotheses = enumerate_hypotheses over BOTH family labellings with
       bound = 8.0 * max(frame.shape[:2]), exactly as `g362_fit_validate._search` calls it. Each
       becomes H = getPerspectiveTransform(court_quad, image_quad), normalised by H[2, 2].
    5. SYMMETRY IMAGES: for every enumerated H and every S in (X, R, Y), H S joins the candidate
       set. H S draws the same image ink under a different court labelling.
    6. Candidates are deduplicated on tuple(round(v, 6) for v in H.ravel()).
    7. SCORE: `g362_fit_validate.whole_template_score(H, field, ARM_A)`, imported unchanged -- the
       soft chamfer over the WHOLE 398-point template with the out-of-frame points kept in the
       denominator. A self-fit residual is never a score in this row (B8).
    8. GATE: `g334_court_line_calibration.gate` on every candidate, exactly as G362 applies it
       inside the search. Gate-invalid candidates are counted, reported and never ranked.
    9. Ranking is by score descending, ties by dedupe key ascending, so the order is deterministic.
       The sealed M = 8 highest-ranked are refined with G365 candidate B, `g365_refine_b.refine_b`,
       over the FIT supports with their parent stroke lengths, its constants imported unchanged:
       BAND_B_PX 6.0, HUBER_DELTA_B_PX 1.0, ROUNDS_B 3, CONV_B_PX 0.01, WEIGHT_CLIP (0.5, 2.0),
       MAX_ITER 200, TOL 1e-10. No G365 constant is changed here.
   10. Each refined candidate is re-scored on the same field; its labelled gap, modulo gap and
       attaining element are recorded.

Two SEPARATE questions are then answered per geometry, and the memo keeps them apart:
    REACHABILITY  winner_modulo = the refined candidate with the smallest modulo gap (ties by higher
                  re-score, then key): does the sealed search space CONTAIN a within-bar solution the
                  refiner can reach. Selecting it by gap makes it an upper bound on reachability,
                  NEVER a claim that a fitter could pick it without knowing the truth.
    SELECTABILITY margin = score(top-1 by score) minus score(the highest-scoring refined candidate
                  NOT symmetry-equivalent to the top-1); A and B are symmetry-equivalent when
                  min over S of median || project(A S, POINTS) - project(B, POINTS) || <= SYM_EQ_PX.

Sealed: SYM_EQ_PX = 2.0 px; AMBIG_BAND = 0.005 in absolute units of the whole-template objective,
which is a mean soft chamfer in [0, 1] over 398 points, so 0.005 is worth two points of complete
agreement. margin <= AMBIG_BAND, or no distinct candidate existing at all, yields AMBIGUOUS --
never VALID. margin > AMBIG_BAND yields SELECTABLE. The per-geometry cell printed is
`<REACHED|NOT_REACHED>/<SELECTABLE|AMBIGUOUS>`, and both halves appear in `search.csv` and the memo.

## 7. Orientation set -- pinning, sampling, blind raters, adjudication
Source pinning (G361). A section is identified by its YouTube video id, its start offset in seconds,
the byte size of the fetched container and that container SHA-256, all carried into
`orientation.csv`. A game id alone is NOT a source and makes a section ineligible (A9). The finisher
fetches each selected section with `/workspace/YTDLP_RECIPE.txt` (rung 270, `--download-sections`,
output template `%(id)s_s%(section_start)d.%(ext)s`, which reproduces the section id exactly).

Sampling, sealed and even. Games are ordered by sha256("G367-2026-09-09|" + game) and sections
within a game by the same digest of the section; selection is round-robin over games taking at most
MAX_PER_GAME = 2 per game until SECTIONS_TARGET = 20 sections are held, so at least 10 games
contribute and the >= 20 sections / >= 8 games floor holds by construction. FRAMES_PER_SECTION = 3,
giving 60 frames. Within a section of n decoded frames drawing k: spacing = n // k,
start = max(digest(section) % spacing, 2), indices = start + i * spacing; spacing below 4 raises
rather than sampling, so a head slice is impossible (A3, B7). Each planned frame and its two causal
neighbours are decoded in one sequential read, never a seek, and cached as PNG in pod scratch. Frame
identity is sha256(b"G367|" + shape + b"|" + frame.tobytes()), never a frame number joined to an
archived table.

Sheets. One JPEG per frame, at most 200 KB, named by the first 12 hex of the frame key: the frame at
exactly 960 px wide above a three-frame strip (t-2, t-1, t). It carries NO cue value, NO cue name, NO
detector output, NO section id and no burned-in text of any kind; the builder module has no
text-drawing call and imports no cue, and the test asserts both.

Raters. Two blind raters, `terra` and `sol`, each see only a sheet and this fixed instruction, and
each writes one row of `ratings.csv` (`frame_key,rater,label,reason`):

    Look at the large top image; the strip below is the two frames before it, for motion only.
    Answer for the LARGE image. Which way is the team in possession attacking? Label `left` if they
    are attacking the basket toward the left of the image, `right` if toward the right, `unknown` if
    you cannot tell. Give a one-line reason.

Where the two raters agree, that label is the reference. Where they disagree, a third blind
adjudicator (same instruction, same sheet, no rater labels shown) writes a row with rater
`ADJUDICATOR` and that row is the reference. A frame with neither agreement nor an adjudication is
EXCLUDED from every denominator and counted in `unadjudicated_frames` -- named and counted, never
silently dropped (B1). Cohen kappa over the two primary raters is reported.

## 8. Sealed cue candidates and how they are scored
Four cues, each a deterministic function of the cached frame alone, each returning `left`, `right`
or `unknown`. No cue reads a label, a rating or another cue, so no cue can be circular. All four run
on a 960-wide working copy of the frame.
    C1 basket_side   near-white compact structure (backboard) mass in the upper 55 pct band, left
                     third against right third. SIGN: attack the side with the LARGER mass, because
                     the attacked basket is the one the camera holds in frame.
    C2 graphic_side  low-saturation extreme-luma ink (score bug) mass in the bottom 22 pct band,
                     left half against right half. SIGN: attack the side OPPOSITE the heavier half,
                     because a bug is placed away from the live action.
    C3 crowd_side    non-floor pixel share (hue farther than 15 from the modal floor hue, or
                     saturation below 40), left third against right third. SIGN: attack the side
                     with the LARGER share, because the camera frames the attacked end with stands.
    C4 pan_direction phase correlation between the 128x72 greyscale images of t-1 and t. SIGN:
                     content moving LEFT by at least PAN_MIN means the camera pans right, so the
                     offence attacks `right`; moving RIGHT by PAN_MIN means `left`; else `unknown`.

Sealed thresholds: RATIO_MARGIN = 1.15 for C1, C2 and C3 (a side wins only when its mass exceeds the
other side's by that factor, otherwise `unknown`); PAN_MIN = 1.5 px measured at 128 px width for C4.

Every sign is sealed NOW, before any frame is opened. A sign chosen after seeing labels would be a
fit: the finisher reports the precision of the SEALED sign and prints the complement beside it,
labelled a post-hoc diagnostic that is NOT a sealed result of this row.

    N_labelled  = adjudicated frames whose reference is `left` or `right` (never the count of frames
                  a cue happened to fire on -- contract H1)
    predicted   = frames of N_labelled where the cue returned `left` or `right`
    precision   = correct / predicted, with the two-sided Wilson 95 pct interval
    abstention  = 1 - predicted / N_labelled
    per class   = n_left and n_right; either below 30 makes the orientation part PARTIAL (Q7)

The best cue is REPORTED. Nothing is wired: no `src/` hook, no flag, no change to
`line_calibration.py`, no use of any cue in the geometry part.
