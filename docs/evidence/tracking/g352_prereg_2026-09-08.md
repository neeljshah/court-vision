## Objective and gates

For each candidate feet-to-image H, project all 398 NBA-template points. Its
forward loss is the mean of `min(distance_to_fit_support, 48.0)` over all 398
points. A projected point outside the measurement image contributes 48.0. Its
reverse loss is the mean of `min(distance_to_projected_template, 48.0)` over all
held-out physical supports; the symmetric loss is their arithmetic mean. Lower is
better. The 48.0-pixel penalty is fixed here; it is eight times G334's sealed
6.0-pixel soft-chamfer half-width and is never selected by a scored section.

Each hypothesis is rejected inside the search before ranking if it has fewer than
100 in-frame template points, a non-finite or singular matrix, a reflected or
folded projected half-court quadrilateral, projected-half-court area below 0.02 of
the image, median local scale outside [4.0, 60.0] pixels per foot, or fewer than
two observed orientation families separated by at least 20 degrees. The retained
winner is the minimum symmetric loss. Runner-up margin is runner_up_loss minus
winner_loss; positive means the winner has lower loss.

## Holdout and arms

Before search, group detected segments into whole physical supports by the landed
G334 collinearity grouping. Deterministically reserve at least 25 percent of those
supports by SHA-256 ordering of `section|frame|support_index|352`; a support is
wholly FIT or wholly HELDOUT, never split. The same HELDOUT supports score ARM_A,
ARM_B, and ROUTE for a frame. ARM_A uses the landed G334 extraction parameters with
this objective. ARM_B changes only to G334 ARM_B's sealed LSD threshold and keeps
the same objective. ROUTE is the production homography on this same heldout set.
