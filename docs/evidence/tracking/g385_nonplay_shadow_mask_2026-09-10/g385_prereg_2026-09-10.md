## Fixed method

The development-only model is L2 logistic regression over the frozen G375
ResNet-18 embeddings: C=1, class_weight=balanced, solver=liblinear,
random_state=385, max_iter=1000. Training targets PLAY versus definite
NONPLAY/OTHER_SPORT/NON_SPORT. UNKNOWN is excluded from training only and is
listed in `model_identity.json`. The finisher freezes model bytes,
preprocessing, source digests, and model SHA-256 before validation inference.

The proposed mask is SHADOW-only: `p_nonplay >= 0.95` yields MASK; every other
readable tick yields KEEP; missing or invalid evidence yields UNKNOWN and
passes through. It does not write a production flag, section mask, ledger
field, registry field, or anything under `data/`.

After the premise, use only development-disjoint candidates. Draw exactly 30
preserved sections from at least 10 videos, evenly over competition, video,
and time. Each section contributes 12 strictly interior ticks for 360 planned
keys. Pin pixels before blind rating. Terra and sol independently rate native
frames PLAY/NONPLAY/OTHER_SPORT/NON_SPORT/UNKNOWN without probabilities or
proposed decisions. Preserve raw disagreements and adjudicate blind.

All 360 attempted keys remain in `frames.csv`; no top-up follows labels. The
future score retains UNKNOWN in both planned-frame and reference denominators:
false masks / all reference PLAY; captured masks / all definite non-play;
UNKNOWN / all planned frames. The sign convention for any later delta is
improvement = baseline loss minus candidate loss; positive means candidate
better. This row has no scored comparison in this commit.

The descriptive exact-ID control is limited to the known G375 soccer video;
it is not a learned sport classifier and cannot validate sport rejection.

## Fixed bars and boundaries

The fixed bars are at least 30 PLAY and 30 definite non-play, Wilson 95 upper
false-mask rate at most 0.05, Wilson 95 lower non-play capture at least 0.50,
all 360 attempts represented, and zero masks from absent evidence. A class
below 30 is PARTIAL; an insufficient preserved/disjoint supply is NOT
VALIDATED. No live feeder, daemon, content gate, source table, G374 threshold,
mask, flag, label, register, or ledger changes. Any integration is a proposed
diff only. The finisher runs ratings on the PC under the RAM gate and frozen
head inference on the pod GPU one job at a time; this preparation runs neither.
