# G284 Pass 1 blind protocol

This sealed Pass 1 opens only the 61 unmarked JPEGs in `../g278_census_stratified_followup_artifact/part_a/frames/`. It does not open G267's records, a detector overlay, a detector count, G278's labels, or any joined result.

The supplied G278 blind order is retained verbatim as this pass's randomized presentation order. A frame is COUNTED only when its painted boundary lines show enough of the court of play to judge whether a person's location is inside the boundary. PLAYERS are visibly identifiable active players inside those lines. OTHER PEOPLE are visibly identifiable non-players (for example, officials) whose location is inside those same lines. Bench, sideline, crowd, and any person outside the boundary are excluded. `CANNOT_COUNT` means the frame does not show enough court to make that inside-boundary call; its numeric fields are intentionally blank.

Counts are of people visibly on court, not inferred roster positions. A fully occluded person is not countable. This is a coarse visual count, not the sub-pixel geometric measurement bounded by G257.
