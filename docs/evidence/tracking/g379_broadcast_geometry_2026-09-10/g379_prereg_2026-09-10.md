## 3. SCREEN (fixed here, BEFORE any fit)
3.1 For every section of the screen population, the 12 tick indices are
`g364_sampler.interior_indices(frame_count, 12)` -- strictly interior, evenly spaced, never a head
slice -- with `frame_count` taken from the pinned ffprobe stream. A section whose `frame_count` is
too small for 12 unique interior indices is `TOO_SHORT` and leaves the screen population, counted.
3.2 Each tick frame is decoded at NATIVE resolution and embedded by the FROZEN route, imported and
unmodified: `scripts/platformkit/tracking/g364_embedder.embeddings` (torchvision ResNet-18,
`weights_sha256 f37072fd47e89c5e827621c5baffa7500819f7896bbacec160b1a16c560e07ec`, 224x224
INTER_AREA, ImageNet mean/std, 512-dim average pool), then
`scripts/platformkit/tracking/g364_predict.predict` with the frozen head
`docs/evidence/tracking/g364_learned_court_presence_2026-09-09/model.json`.
3.3 The frozen threshold is `0.30` and IS NOT MOVED. Its landed meaning, quoted from
`scripts/platformkit/tracking/g364_train.decisions`, is the TOP-TWO PROBABILITY MARGIN: a frame is
`ABSTAIN` when that margin is below the threshold, else `COURT` when the argmax class is
`USABLE_COURT` and `NON_COURT` otherwise. The spec's phrase "presence probability >= 0.30" is read
as this landed rule; no other reading is used and no bar changes.
3.4 A section is ELIGIBLE when the head returns `COURT` on at least 0.50 of its 12 ticks
(>= 6 of 12). `ABSTAIN` and `NON_COURT` ticks are counted and are not `COURT`.
3.5 The SIX sections are drawn EVENLY over the eligible sections with a fixed seed, by this rule and
no other: order the eligible sections by `(game_id, section_id)`; group them by `game_id`; visit the
games in an order fixed by `sha256("G379|" + game_id)` ascending (this is the seed, the literal
string `G379`); take at most one section per game per pass, choosing within a game the section at
`interior_indices`-style even position `(pass_index * (n_sections - 1)) // max(1, passes - 1)` when
`n_sections > 1`, else its only section; repeat passes until six sections are chosen. If the
eligible set holds fewer than 4 distinct games or fewer than 6 sections, the screen clause is
UNMET: the row proceeds on what exists, every count is printed, and the verdict is PARTIAL naming
this clause. NOTHING in this draw reads a fit, a residual, a stroke count or a rater label.
3.6 The realized screen (`screen.csv`, `source_identity.csv`) is committed BEFORE any fit runs.
3.7 MARGIN AUDIT (`presence_margin_audit.csv`). `g364_predict` emits a legacy `margin` column that
is `probs[-1] - probs[-2]` over the UNSORTED class vector, not the top-two margin the decision uses.
The legacy column is PRESERVED and unmodified; this row adds `top_two_margin` beside it and reports
the reproduction share of each. NO sampling, screening or selection in this row reads either margin
column: the head enters only through its categorical `prediction`.

## 4. DECODE AND STROKE SPLIT (fixed BEFORE fitting)
4.1 Per selected section, the scheduled frames are `g364_sampler.interior_indices(frame_count, 60)`
-- 60 unique, evenly spaced, strictly interior. A scheduled frame that does not decode is
`DECODE_FAILED`, stays in the denominator, and is enumerated.
4.2 Every scheduled frame is resized to 720 rows by `g362_fit_validate.to_base_height` before any
pixel quantity is computed, so every px number below is natively at 720p.
4.3 Strokes are `g362_strokes.extract_strokes(frame720, ARM_A)`; the FIT / VALIDATION split is
`g362_strokes.partition`, unchanged: within each family the `ceil(n/4)` lowest content-addressed
stroke ids are held out. The split is CONTENT-ADDRESSED and carries no random draw, so it is fixed
by the pixels alone and is reproducible from `stroke_split.csv`; that file is written for every
frame BEFORE the fitter is called on that frame, and the fit reads only the FIT side.
4.4 Constants imported and NEVER redefined: `MIN_STROKE_PX 40.0`, `SUPPORT_SPACING_PX 6.0`,
`FAMILY_BIN_DEG 15.0`, `VALIDATION_DIVISOR 4`, `MIN_STROKES 4`, `MIN_FAMILIES 2`,
`MIN_VAL_FAMILIES 2`, `MIN_VAL_POINTS 30`, `MAX_MEDIAN_PX 8.0`, `PENALTY_PX 64.0`,
`BASE_HEIGHT 720`, `BAND_B_PX 6.0`, `HUBER_DELTA_B_PX 1.0`, `ROUNDS_B 3`, `CONV_B_PX 0.01`,
`WEIGHT_CLIP (0.5, 2.0)`, `SYM_EQ_PX 2.0`, `ACCEPT_MARGIN 0.10`, head threshold `0.30`.

## 5. FIT AND SELECTION (the two routes, reported SEPARATELY)
5.1 RAW route, per frame: `g362_fit_validate.decide(frame720)`. This is the landed G362 cascade --
whole-template gated search on the FIT strokes, then ONE held-out decision -- with no presence head
and no G371 selection. Its `state` is reported as `raw_state` in `residuals.csv`.
5.2 CASCADE route, per frame: the frozen presence head of section 3 applied to that frame, then
`g371_symmetry_margin.select_frame(frame720)`, which internally runs the sealed G367 enumeration on
the FIT strokes, refines every candidate with `g365_refine_b.refine_b` and scores it with the
FIT-only objective `g362_fit_validate.whole_template_score`, clusters candidates by
`g367_symmetry.equivalent` at `SYM_EQ_PX 2.0`, and returns `ACCEPT` only when the winner's class
margin is >= `ACCEPT_MARGIN 0.10` AND the held-out decision on the winner is `VALID`.
A refusal is a refusal: `NO_CANDIDATES`, `NO_DISTINCT_RUNNER_UP`, `REFUSED_MARGIN`, `NO_VALIDATION`
and `REFUSED_VALIDATION` are reported as returned. There is NO override and NO oracle: no truth
homography, no VALIDATION stroke and no rater label enters ranking or selection (B8).
5.3 SELECTED MATRIX: the class winner `Selection.winner.matrix` returned by the same call, which
exists whenever any candidate exists, including under a refusal. It is used, unrefitted, for
section 6's validation and section 7's feet. Using it under a refusal is a DIAGNOSTIC and never a
pass: nothing in section 9 counts a refused frame as accepted.
5.4 COMPUTE SHORTFALL: a frame whose CASCADE route exceeds 900 s of wall time is killed and
recorded `TIMEOUT`. `TIMEOUT`, `DECODE_FAILED` and `SOURCE_LOST` stay in every denominator, are
enumerated in `refusals.csv`, and can never be a pass (B3).

## 6. VALIDATION OF THE SELECTED MATRIX (never a refit)
6.1 `validation_status` per scheduled frame, from the landed G362 rule applied to the SELECTED
matrix on the RESERVED strokes only, via `g362_fit_validate.bidirectional` and the constants of 4.4:
- `NO_LINES` -- fewer than 4 strokes or fewer than 2 stroke families in the frame.
- `NO_VALIDATION` -- fewer than 2 held-out families or fewer than 30 held-out fixed-density supports.
- `NO_CANDIDATES` -- the selector returned no candidate, so no matrix exists to validate.
- `REFUSED` -- `max(median(forward), median(inverse)) > MAX_MEDIAN_PX 8.0` at 720p, forward taken
  over the WHOLE 398-point template with out-of-frame points charged `PENALTY_PX 64.0`.
- `VALID` -- that same maximum is <= 8.0 px.
6.2 All five statuses, plus `TIMEOUT`, `DECODE_FAILED` and `SOURCE_LOST`, are kept DISTINCT and all
stay in N (B1). No status replaces or renames a landed one (B2).
6.3 No support point is duplicated: supports are the fixed-density `SUPPORT_SPACING_PX 6.0` points of
whole held-out strokes, and the `>= 30` count is a count of those points with no re-use (B9).

## 7. FEET
7.1 Feet come from the DEPLOYED route, imported read-only from the deploy tree
`/workspace/deploy/nba-ai-system` via PYTHONPATH: `src.tracking.player_detection.FeetDetector`,
called exactly as the landed callers call it -- `model(image, classes=[0], conf=.3, verbose=False,
imgsz=640, half=<cuda>, device=<cuda>)` -- on the SAME 720p frame the geometry used, with one foot
point `((x1 + x2) / 2, y2)` per detected box. No `src/` file is written.
7.2 A foot is mapped by `Selection.winner`'s court matrix (`inv(image_matrix)`, normalised), never
by a refit.
7.3 `feet_inside` = the share of ALL feet detected on ALL scheduled decoded frames of the section
whose mapped point is finite and lies inside the FULL court rectangle, `0 <= x <= 50` ft and
`0 <= y <= 94` ft, in the fitted near-half frame of reference. This rectangle is invariant under the
reflection `y -> 94 - y` that the near-half template does not decide, so `feet_inside` carries NO
orientation claim. Feet on frames with no selected matrix are counted in the DENOMINATOR and are not
inside (B1: the frames that would fail are not excluded).
7.4 `forward_median_px` per section, pooled OVER POINTS: the per-template-point forward residuals of
section 6.1 pooled across every scheduled frame of the section. Two figures are printed side by
side and both appear in `summary.json`:
`forward_median_px_charged` -- frames with no evaluable residual contribute `PENALTY_PX 64.0` on
each of the 398 template points, which is G362's own charge for a point that cannot be scored, so
no frame is excluded; this is the figure the bar of section 9 is read against.
`forward_median_px_evaluable` -- the same median over evaluable frames only, with the excluded count
and share named beside it.

## 8. NEGATIVES
8.1 POOL: 20 tick indices per section by `g364_sampler.interior_indices(frame_count, 20)` over at
least 30 distinct pinned sections. The pinned sections of section 1 are used first; if fewer than 30
are available the corpus is re-censused at 30-minute intervals and newly arrived sections are pinned
the same way, until at least 30 distinct sections are held. The pool manifest is sealed and
committed BEFORE any sheet is rendered and BEFORE any rating is read.
8.2 Blind rating: sheets are rendered from the pool manifest by the unchanged G364 renderer
`scripts/platformkit/tracking/g364_sheets`, carrying no prediction, probability, margin, geometry or
label. Raters `terra` and `sol` label every sheet in the frozen G364 vocabulary
(`USABLE_COURT`, `CLOSEUP`, `CROWD_GRAPHICS`, `UNKNOWN`) in batches of at most 40 under the RAM
gate. Disagreements are adjudicated blind from the sheets alone; an agreed pair is never overridden.
8.3 NEGATIVES = every pool frame whose adjudicated reference label is `CLOSEUP` or
`CROWD_GRAPHICS`. `USABLE_COURT` and `UNKNOWN` frames are NOT negatives and are reported separately.
The negatives clause needs >= 200 such frames from >= 30 sections; if the pool yields fewer, the
count is reported as measured, the clause is PARTIAL, and NO frame is added after any label or any
cascade outcome is read (no post-score quota filling).
8.4 The CASCADE of 5.2 is run on EVERY negative, including those the head already calls `NON_COURT`,
and must ACCEPT ZERO of them. An acceptance is `prediction == COURT` AND `geometry_status == ACCEPT`
AND `validation_status == VALID`. `NO_LINES`, `NO_VALIDATION`, `NO_CANDIDATES`, `REFUSED`,
`REFUSED_MARGIN`, `NO_DISTINCT_RUNNER_UP` and `TIMEOUT` on a negative are counted as themselves and
NEVER as passes; they are also never counted as refusals earned by geometry when the head alone
stopped the frame -- both stages are reported per frame.

## 9. METRIC, BEFORE, BAR, N (byte-identical to the spec's ACCEPTANCE RULE)
metric = per section: VALID share of scheduled decoded frames; feet_inside share; forward residual
median (720p); raw fitter vs presence-cascade outcomes SEPARATELY; refusal statuses per frame;
negatives accepted / 200
before = G352 0/4 measured (six required); G362 broadcast stages never run; G371 0 accepts on
synthetic
bar = six sections / >= 4 games, 60 unique decoded frames and >= 100 feet EACH; >= 4/6 sections with
VALID >= 0.50 AND feet_inside >= 0.60 AND forward median <= 8 px; cascade accepts 0/200 negatives;
every G362 validity constant unchanged; 0 refits; the six-section conclusion is descriptive (PARTIAL
for any sampled section-level claim)
n = 6 sections (screen), 360 frames, >= 100 feet each; negatives >= 200 frames / >= 30 sections;
>= 30 frames per scored stratum, else descriptive
The VALID share denominator is named in every row and in `summary.json`: the SCHEDULED decoded
frames of that section, 60 by construction, never the count of frames that happen to carry a fit
(H1). `VALID` in the bar is `validation_status == VALID` of section 6.1. The cascade acceptance
share is reported beside it and is a different, named number.
A bar found unmeetable is reported at its measured value and CLOSED AT LIMIT or PARTIAL; it is
never lowered (Q3, B10).
