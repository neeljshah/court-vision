## Why this amendment exists

Prereg section 5 requires "the G370 M1 scoring share ... restated over
`producer_evaluated_ticks`" but never writes the NUMERATOR down. The landed implementation
therefore divided the RAW distinct-frame count of `tracking_data.csv` (`observed_ticks`, `|T|`)
by `|E|`, which is neither an M1 quantity nor an intersection. The rule is fixed here, exactly,
before it is measured.

## A1.1 The corrected quantity (binding)

Per section let:

- `E` -- the producer-evaluated frame set, the reconstructed schedule of prereg section 2
  (`ball_tracking.csv` rows with `live = 1`). Unchanged by this amendment.
- `M1` -- the G370 M1 PLAYER frame set, obtained by IMPORTING the landed G370 route and copying
  none of it: `load_merged(tracking, ball)` -> `adapt_production_section` ->
  `construct_arm(table, "A0")` -> `collapse_held(...)[0]` -> rows with `cls == "player"`, taking
  the distinct integral `frame` values. This is byte-for-byte the construction
  `scripts/platformkit/tracking/g370_scorer.py:104-117` performs, and
  `scripts/platformkit/tracking/g376_premise.py:27-32` already implements it.

The M1-corrected quantities are then, per section:

- `m1_observed_ticks_in_producer_evaluated` = `|M1 & E|`
- `m1_zero_ticks_over_producer_evaluated`  = `|E| - |M1 & E|`
- `m1_zero_share_over_producer_evaluated`  = `(|E| - |M1 & E|) / |E|`; ABSENT where `|E| = 0` or
  the section has no reconstructed schedule.

Set totals aggregate `sum |E|` and `sum (|E| - |M1 & E|)` over the sections that HAVE a
reconstructed schedule and divide once. A section without one contributes to neither; it is
already UNKNOWN under prereg section 3, and this run has 0 such sections.

## A1.2 The RAW columns keep their names and their values (B2)

`observed_ticks`, `zero_share_over_declared` and `zero_share_over_producer_evaluated` are RAW

them, in `consequence.csv` and in `summary.json`.
