## Design (sealed section 3 to 6), and the one thing it changes against G210
Extraction reuses `g123.enhance_contrast`, `line_calibration.detect_lsd_segments` and `g132.union_segments` under `g210._groups`' composition. Segments are split into two families by length-weighted angle modes at least 20 deg apart; each family's collinear groups are kept TOP_K longest. G210 measured 0/17 with 2,048 uniform draws and G210b 0/17 with 16,384, recording that the correct groups were in the pool every time and no draw ever contained them, while G217 measured that the solver and court model contribute 0.000000 px. The ONE change here is therefore the SAMPLER: the structured hypothesis set is enumerated COMPLETELY, with no random draw in the search. Four line intersections give `cv2.getPerspectiveTransform`; scoring is a soft chamfer against the distance transform of the FIT half; the winner is smoothed by an elementwise median over the frames of its 5-frame shot run and re-gated on the centre frame.
