## Blinded packet and ratings

Raters receive opaque contexts and native tiles only: no old polygons, old clicks, matrices, labels, or one another's output. Each independently records at most two straight physical markings in fixed opaque order. For each visible marking, record physical ID, two endpoints, and an independently clicked interior midpoint in native coordinates. Use the longest contiguous visible segment; a tie is topmost then leftmost. Occlusion ends a segment. Do not bridge, infer a template, retry, or recalibrate.

For every context each rater records `VISIBLE`, `ABSENT`, or `UNKNOWN` and a reason. An empty list is allowed only with retained `ABSENT` or `UNKNOWN`. Displayed tile size and offset are retained. The parser requires an observation for every opaque context and preserves uncertainty in the denominator.

## Known-position controls

Use the same 30 backgrounds. Add one white 6 px straight band over a black 10 px underlay, native length 240 px. Orientation cycles 0, 30, 60 degrees. Centre cycles the six tile centres. Endpoint order is fixed. Seed 387 permutes opaque presentation order. The canonical known-point SHA-256 is `68d720b5fab79d66bc93cb0d4e1e409b76ad9985c038c1fd97afc228f68d051f`.

Controls are rated only after sealed real labels, with the same protocol and no feedback. A control passes only when both raters have endpoint error at most 3 native px after allowing reversal and each midpoint is at most 3 px from the known band centreline. Missing clicks fail. Controls are reported as `success/30` and are never entered into the real-frame denominator.

## Pairing, adjudication, and bars

Pair real IDs once. A same-ID pair needs endpoints within 6 native px after allowing reversal, plus each midpoint within 3 px of both reported segments. The adjudicator first records `YES`, `NO`, or `UNKNOWN` visibility with no traces displayed. It then audits nine evenly spaced positions per paired trace, including endpoints; each must lie within 3 native px of visible physical paint. A real frame localizes only if at least one pair passes and both traces have at least 8/9 audited positions. Unmatched, missing, `ABSENT`, and `UNKNOWN` frames remain in the 30-frame real denominator.

The fixed bars are: input identity requires all 60 original states retained in accounting and all 30 selected real contexts and 30 controls attempted; known-position localization requires at least 27/30 controls; real localization requires at least 24/30 real contexts plus the control bar; reproducibility requires byte-identical canonical rendering and scoring from two fresh processes. There are no subgroup bars.
