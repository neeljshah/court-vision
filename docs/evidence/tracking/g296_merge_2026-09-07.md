## 7. Honest limitations
Two model locators agreeing measures REPRODUCIBILITY, never CORRECTNESS, and both can be wrong in the same way -- and the adjudicator that broke every tie is a third model, so an accepted point is not human ground truth in the strict sense. No human has checked these frames. G291 measured two model raters at Cohen's kappa 0.283439 (47/72 raw) on an easier four-category task; that rater sensitivity is inherited here and is why this merge reports a distance distribution rather than one agreement number. 24 frames fix the span-representativeness problem and do NOT fix the one-clip problem.
