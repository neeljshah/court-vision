## 1. Binding premise

Run `premise` against every nonblank row of the live ledger snapshot, with `finished_at`
at or after `2026-09-09T17:47:00Z`, and scan each source directory one at a time for a
same-`game_id` MP4. Print G361's `summary.json` class counts and the count of its
`ledger_links.csv` rows with non-UNKNOWN `deploy_manifest_sha256`. Print
`fresh_sections_with_source=<n>`. If n is less than 10, the prospective premise is FALSE:
write the memo with this exact output and stop. No re-sampling substitutes for a false
premise.

## 2. PIN rule and required binding fields

Eligible sections are fresh ledger rows with a parseable source id, requested start offset,
and an MP4 still on disk. Sort by `game_id`; if n is at most 40, take all; otherwise retain
the complete every-`floor(n/30)` sequence, never truncate it. This fixed rule spans the
eligible set and records every selected attempt, including ABSENT with its error. The bar is
at least 30 attempted fresh sections, at least 10 games, and 3 competitions; lower coverage
is PARTIAL and is named, not redrawn.

Every `sources.csv` row must contain: `game_id`, `source_id`, `competition`,
`requested_start_s`, `source_path`, `source_sha256`, `source_bytes`, `codec`, `fps`,
`width`, `height`, `nb_frames`, `first_pts`, `crop_rule`, `pixel_transform`,
`producer_config`, and `ledger_row_json`. `attempts.csv` names every selected candidate with
status `PINNED` or `ABSENT` and an error text. `manifests.json` records SHA-256 plus bytes
for the exercised routes `src/pipeline/unified_pipeline.py`,
`src/tracking/ball_detect_track.py`, `scripts/run_clip.py`, and weights `yolov8n.pt`,
`yolov8n_ball.pt`, `osnet_x0_25_imagenet.pth`. Crop provenance is `TOPCUT = 60` at
`src/tracking/video_handler.py:11`, applied as `frame[TOPCUT:]` at line 61 and in the
producer route; pixel coordinates are therefore (x, y-60) after this crop.

## 3. Alignment, repeatability, collisions, and eye check

Original source bytes pinned with their current ledger row are EXACT by construction; state
that in `alignment.csv`. A re-fetch join instead compares at least 30 evenly selected
distinct archived ticks per section using G361 `align`; every absolute temporal error must
be at most 1 native frame. The error sign is re-fetched presentation stamp minus archived
tick timestamp in native frames; positive means the re-fetched decode is later.

Run G369's deterministic export twice against identical inputs. Archive both SHA-256 values
in `export_hashes.json`; unequal bytes are PARTIAL and the line-level difference is reported.
Group parsed `(source_id, requested_start_s)` claims by distinct table `game_id`; two or more
are collisions. Parse failures are reported separately. Render 30 evenly selected strips
with G361 `g361_strips`, each at most 200 KB; no head slice is permitted.
