## 0. Scope and sign convention

This is an additive admission-record preparation, not a calibration conclusion.
The finisher first re-runs the exact G370 premise on 59 fresh plus 34 sealed
sections. A positive delta means baseline loss minus candidate loss, so positive
means the candidate is better. Rejection shares are not losses: lower usable
control rejection is better and higher identifiable-plant detection is better.
No threshold, flag, daemon, `src/`, `kernel/`, `api/`, `intel/`, `data/`,
`data/registry/`, register, or ledger is changed.

## 1. Admission schema and missing-evidence behavior

Each `admission_rows.parquet` record represents one scheduled decoded frame. It
contains nullable provenance: `source_id`, `source_uri`, requested start/end,
`source_sha256`, `byte_size`, `frame_sha256`, `table_sha256`,
`source_identity_status`, `alignment_error_native_frames`,
`deploy_manifest_sha256`, `route_sha256`, and `weights_sha256`; clock/image:
`game_id`, `competition`, `section_id`, `frame_index`, `pts`, `timebase`,
`decoded_frame_count`, `evaluated_tick_id`, `evaluated_tick_verified`, `width`,
`height`, `crop_xywh`, `pixel_transform_sha256`, `split`, and
`inclusion_probability`; observation arrays for tracks and ball; calibration
fields; and decision fields described by G370 METHOD 1.

Track items include `track_id`, `track_generation`, `bbox_px`, `xy_px`,
`position_source`, `producer_branch_file_line`, `attribution_evidence_sha256`,
`last_observed_pts`, and `age_s`. Ball items use the same source fields plus
`observed_box_px`. `position_source` is exactly DETECTION, PREDICTION, HELD, or
UNKNOWN. `court_presence_label`, `court_presence_probability`,
`court_presence_model_sha256`, `geometry_status`, `orientation_status`,
`symmetry_class`, `H_sha256`, `template_sha256`, `template_status`,
`validation_support_sha256`, `residual_px`, and `uncertainty_m` remain nullable.
`ball_x2d_px` and `ball_y2d_px` are nullable input fields: absent on archived
tables and available on newer producer tables. Decision values retain M0 and M1
gate scores, `gate_status_by_task`, `task_mask`, `reason_codes`,
`evidence_sha256s`, `admission_spec_sha256`, `admission_status`, and `scorable`.

An absent input becomes ABSENT or UNKNOWN and disables only its corresponding
task mask. It never creates a bad-frame quarantine. Image-only geometry is not
scorable. PREDICTION, HELD, and UNKNOWN are never labelled observed.

## 2. Frozen v0 gate set and evaluated-tick representation

M1 is the only scored representation: production rows are adapted, then held
player positions are collapsed using imported `g359_held_position`; ball rows are
never collapsed. M0 diagnostics remain beside M1. The v0 set is the two confirmed
artifact gates `zero_step_share` and `distinct_position_ratio`, plus frozen and
id-merge detection controls constructed by imported `g358_gate_execution`.
`stationary_track_share` is diagnostic only. No v2 candidate is adopted.

Every future scored tick uses exactly one evaluator state, keyed stably by
`game_id:section_id:frame_index:evaluated_tick_id`; a section may contribute many
states and is never substituted for its ticks. If an outcome comparison is made,
the finisher calls `walk_forward` with its purge and symmetric nonzero embargo,
archives evaluator records, and uses only M1 verified evaluated ticks.

## 3. Controls, plants, bars, and deterministic export

Before reading any admission score, terra and sol independently blind-rate at
least 30 fresh sections across at least 10 games as usable court play. The usable
control rejection bar is at most 0.05 per enabled task. At least 30 independently
applied corruptions per type are constructed before adaptation: frozen, coast, and
id-merge. Identifiable plant detection is at least 0.80. A plant lacking an
identifiable task is reported as such and does not silently enter the denominator.
An unmet bar disables that task mask; it is not a software failure and does not
move a bar. The finisher writes every per-section or per-tick decision to
`decisions.csv` and names the denominator in `denominator_census.csv`.

Two exports on byte-identical input must be byte-identical; both hashes are
stored in `export_hashes.json`. Thirty strips are selected evenly across the
decision ordering, including refused and unknown cases, and each remains at most
200 KB. The finisher archives source path, byte size, and resolution for each
opened input and route identities for each pod-measured row.
