SOURCE-TAG PREFIX. For each population `game_id`: if it matches `^([a-z][a-z_]{2,})-`

ELIGIBILITY AND EXCLUSIONS. A section is ELIGIBLE when its `game_id` parses as an
eleven-character YouTube id plus a numeric section offset by the landed G364 rule
(`scripts/platformkit/tracking/g364_census.py:parse_unit`: `game_id` contains `_s`,
the text after the final `_s` is all digits, and the stem is at least eleven
characters, whose last eleven characters are the video id), AND the snapshot row
carries a positive `source_duration`. Every other population key is EXCLUDED, listed
in `excluded.csv` with its prefix and a reason string, and can never be sampled: a
section with no YouTube id cannot be re-fetched, and an NBA game id alone is never a
source (A9). Excluded keys are counted in the census but not in the sampling frame,
and the memo states both counts.

SAMPLE (n = 300 sections, SAMPLED). Let E be the eligible frame and E_p its members
with prefix p, ordered by `game_id` ascending.
 1. Floor: `base_p = min(10, |E_p|)`.
 2. Remainder `R = 300 - sum(base_p)` is distributed over prefixes by largest
    remainder on `R * (|E_p| - base_p) / sum(|E_q| - base_q)`, capped at
    `|E_p| - base_p`, ties broken by prefix name ascending, repeating until R is
    exhausted or every prefix is capped. `quota_p = base_p + share_p`.
 3. Within prefix p draw evenly with a seeded start, never a head slice (A3/B7):
    `step = |E_p| / quota_p`, `index_i = floor((i + 0.375) * step)` for
    i = 0 .. quota_p - 1, clamped to `|E_p| - 1`. Seed 375; the 0.375 offset is that
    seed. When `quota_p == |E_p|` every member is taken.
 4. If the eligible frame cannot yield 300 the verdict is PARTIAL and the shortfall
    is named; no prefix is topped up by hand and no key is substituted.
The drawn 300 are written to `sample.csv` with prefix, video id, offset, sealed
target tick and opaque sheet id.

SHEET. For each drawn section the sealed target tick is
`t = offset_s + int(source_duration // 2)` seconds of the source video, the
section's midpoint. One container is fetched with
`yt-dlp -f 270/312/137/best[height>=720]/best --no-playlist --no-warnings
--download-sections *T1-T2 --merge-output-format mp4` where `T1 = max(0, t - 2)` and
`T2 = T1 + 5`, and is pinned per G361 with video id, requested window, absolute pod
path, byte size, container SHA-256, width, height, decoded frame count, frame rate,
codec and fetch UTC in `reference.csv`. The sheet frame is the container's frame at
index `floor(nb_frames / 2)`; its presentation timestamp is recorded. The frame is
written as a single JPEG, no overlay, no text, no strip, resized to width 1280
preserving aspect, quality stepped down through 85, 75, 65, 55, 45 until the file is
at or below 200 KB. A section whose fetch or decode fails is ABSENT, recorded in
`excluded.csv` with reason `fetch_failed` or `decode_failed`, is NOT replaced, and
reduces the rated denominator, which the memo states.

BLIND SHEET IDENTITY. Sheet basenames are opaque and carry no source tag, video id,
offset, prefix or sport: `sheet_0000.jpg` .. `sheet_0299.jpg`. The assignment is a
`random.Random(375).shuffle` over the 300 drawn keys sorted by `game_id`, so
neighbouring sheet numbers are not neighbouring sections and the ordering leaks no
prefix. The mapping lives only in `sample.csv`, never in a rater batch.

THE FIVE LABELS. `BASKETBALL_PLAY`, `BASKETBALL_NONPLAY`, `OTHER_SPORT`,
`NON_SPORT`, `UNKNOWN`. No sixth label is accepted; an unparseable or unknown token
from a rater is recorded verbatim in the raw batch file and mapped to `UNKNOWN` in
`ratings.csv`, and the count of such tokens is reported.

ADJUDICATION. Where the two raters' labels for a sheet differ, the finisher opens
that sheet alone, by its opaque id, with no access to its prefix, video id, offset
or either rater's label, and assigns the final label from the same five. Where they
agree, that label is final with no review. Every adjudicated sheet is listed in
`raters/adjudication.csv`. Cohen kappa over the five categories is computed on the
two raters' labels BEFORE adjudication, on the sheets both rated.

METRIC. `impurity_share = (final label != BASKETBALL_PLAY) / (rated sheets)`, with
Wilson 95 percent bounds, overall and per prefix, reported as per-mille integers.
`OTHER_SPORT` and `NON_SPORT` counts are named separately, with the sport names
given. A prefix with fewer than 30 rated sheets is reported DESCRIPTIVE and no bound
is claimed for it (Q7).
