# G126 G111 basketball label-to-source audit

**Verdict: (a) G111's labels are wrong on this audit sample.** The source
decodes and the committed renders agree on all 45 reviewed frames, while the
retained G111 label agrees with them on only 22. This is therefore not a
label-to-render association failure. The G111 reachability headline needs
orchestrator revision; this row does not revise it.

This audit follows [`VERIFIER_CONTRACT.md`](VERIFIER_CONTRACT.md), including
the A7 evidence check and B1-B10 self-check below.

## Blind sample and source-first review

The fixed G111 set contains 59 `none`, 14 `some`, and 147 `four` visible
paint-corner label rows. Before opening any selected label or render, seed
`1261112026` retained all 14 scarce `some` rows and took an independent seeded
sample of 16 `none` and 15 `four` rows, for 45 distinct G111
`(clip, source_frame, slot)` units. This is label-stratified rather than a
head slice and includes all three required strata.

Each selected frame was decoded read-only from the source clip on the pod at
its fixed manifest index. The source-only protocol and the pre-comparison
blind decisions are committed in
[`g126_label_audit/blind_review_protocol.md`](g126_label_audit/blind_review_protocol.md),
[`blind_selection_manifest.csv`](g126_label_audit/blind_selection_manifest.csv),
and [`blind_source_judgements.csv`](g126_label_audit/blind_source_judgements.csv).
Commit `2fcaf084b462e8fa026f2e6e6b1d82583b0b2156` predates the label and render
comparison. All 45 source JPEGs and five contact sheets are under
[`g126_label_audit/blind_source_decodes/`](g126_label_audit/blind_source_decodes/).

## Three-way result

After that commit, the selected G111 labels were joined by their complete
`(clip, source_frame, slot)` key and the matching committed render was viewed.
The source and render were judged using the identical no-extrapolation paint
corner definition. The per-frame three-way table is
[`g126_label_audit/three_way_comparison.csv`](g126_label_audit/three_way_comparison.csv);
the committed-render review sheets are under
[`g126_label_audit/committed_render_review/`](g126_label_audit/committed_render_review/).

| Comparison | Agreement | Wilson 95 pct interval |
|---|---:|---:|
| Source-decoded judgement vs G111 label | 22/45 = 48.9 pct | 35.0--63.0 pct |
| Source-decoded judgement vs committed render | 45/45 = 100.0 pct | 92.1--100.0 pct |
| G111 label vs committed render | 22/45 = 48.9 pct | 35.0--63.0 pct |
| All three agree | 22/45 = 48.9 pct | 35.0--63.0 pct |

The source/render match on every row rules out explanation (b) for this
sample. Explanation (c) is also ruled out: the G111 label column contains the
same paint-corner role vocabulary and its counts are exactly the retained 0,
2, or 4 roles. The direct conflict is instead between the retained role label
and what both the source and the committed render show.

The stratified audit's unweighted source result is 10/45 frames with four
visible paint corners. Reweighting its source four-corner shares within the
fixed G111 label strata gives `(59 * 1/16 + 14 * 2/14 + 147 * 7/15) / 220 =
33.8 pct` as a sample-based corrected reachability estimate. It is an audit
estimate, not a wholesale relabel or replacement census. In particular, the
G111 `147/220 = 66.8 pct` REACH headline is not supported by this source-first
audit and requires orchestrator adjudication.
