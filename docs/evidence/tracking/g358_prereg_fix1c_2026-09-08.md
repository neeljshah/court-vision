## Complete-set selection rule (replaces "first six eligible")

Let n be the count of G346 LIVE sections plus eligible POST_EPOCH sections
with at least 150 unique frames, from the binding premise census. If n <= 40,
every eligible section is sealed (the complete set, no sampling). Otherwise
sections are evenly sampled from a fixed, reproducible order (sort by
`game_id` then `section_name`): take every k-th section with
`k = floor(n / 30)`, starting at index 0, continuing until at least 30
sections are selected. No head slice and no random draw is used at any n.

## Standard median / p90 (replaces `g358_gate_execution.py:235-236`)

For a list of measurements, median is `numpy.median` (the mean of the two
middle values when the count is even) and p90 is `numpy.quantile(values,
0.9)` under numpy's default linear interpolation. This replaces the
select-by-index computation (`values[len(values) // 2]` and
`values[int(0.9 * (len(values) - 1))]`), which the verifier found returns a
non-median value for even n (`G358_VERIFY_2026-09-08.md` DIAGNOSIS FAIL;
`g358_gate_execution.py:233-241`). `pandas.Series.median()` and
`pandas.Series.quantile(0.9, interpolation="linear")` are the numpy-default
equivalents and may be used directly on the already-imported `pandas`
dependency.

## reached_n (replaces the aggregate_gates cell that assigns evaluated_n)

`reached_n` for an arm x duration x gate cell counts every record whose
status was produced by an actual `evaluate_image_space` call: `PASS`,
`REJECT`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `MEASURED_NO_BAR`. It is independent of
`evaluated_n`, which counts `PASS`/`REJECT` only (the false-rejection /
detection share denominator). A cell whose arm could not be constructed
(status `UNIDENTIFIABLE`, reason `arm_construction_unavailable`) or whose
evaluator raised (status `REFUSED`) contributes 0 to `reached_n`. This closes
the NEW GAP that `reached_n` was assigned `evaluated_n`, so executed
`MEASURED_NO_BAR` and `NOT_APPLICABLE` cells reported zero reached
(`G358_VERIFY_2026-09-08.md`; `g358_gate_execution.py:179-185`).
