## 0. Scope, sign convention, and what this row is not

This row CONFIRMS, on sections the daemon tracked AFTER the 34 that produced them, the classes G359
assigned to the image-space gates that reject the unchanged arm, and scores the one v2 candidate G359
preregistered and left unscored. It is a calibration-diagnosis row on non-scorable image-pixel rows; it
certifies no court geometry, no tracking quality, no ownership and no forecast; it carries no monetary
interpretation of any kind; every adapter output row keeps `scorable=False`.

Sign convention. On arm A0 (unchanged) a HIGHER rejection share is WORSE -- it is a false rejection, bar
`<= 0.05`. On A1_FROZEN, A2_ID_MERGE, A5_BALL_SHIFT and the stationary plant a HIGHER rejection share is
BETTER -- it is detection, bar `>= 0.80`. A displayed delta is M1 minus M0, so a positive delta on a
detection cell means the collapsed arm detects more. The evaluated-tick ratio is a ratio of counts and
its bar is a two-sided band on the MEDIAN, `0.90 <= median <= 1.10`. Shares carry six decimals; a
per-mille column beside one is `permille = round(1000 * share)`.

## 5. Per-gate A0 confirmation (the headline) and the evaluated-tick audit

Per gate, over the SELECTED fresh sections, arm A0, duration FULL, denominator the sections whose status
for that gate is PASS or REJECT in that mode -- never the count of rows that happen to exist (H1, B9):
`rejection_share_M1 = rejected_n / evaluated_n` with BAR `<= 0.05`, and `rejection_share_M0`, the same
under M0, REPORTED BESIDE IT with no bar. A gate at or under the bar under M1 is CONFIRMED for the class
G359 gave it; a gate above it is NOT CONFIRMED and is named in the verdict, with the bar not lowered and
the threshold not moved. `n_paired`, the count of sections reaching PASS or REJECT in BOTH modes, is
printed beside every pair, and the memo states in the same sentence as the numbers that the two modes
have DIFFERENT denominators (G359 section 4).

EVALUATED-TICK AUDIT. Per selected section, from the adapted table for arm A0 and the snapshot ledger
record of the same section id: `m0_player_frames` = distinct `frame` over PLAYER rows before the
collapse; `m1_player_frames` = the same after `collapse_held`; `ratio_vs_evaluated = m1_player_frames /
ledger_evaluated_frames`; and the additive `ratio_vs_decoded_over_stride = m1_player_frames /
(decoded_frames / stride)`. Ball rows are excluded from both counts: the collapse never touches them, so
including them would force the M1 count to equal the M0 count by construction. The second ratio
cross-checks the same quantity under the other reading of "evaluated_frames / stride"; the FIRST is the
barred one. BAR: the MEDIAN of `ratio_vs_evaluated` over the selected sections lies in `[0.90, 1.10]`. A
section with no ledger record, or a zero or missing `evaluated_frames`, leaves the ratio empty, is named
in `ticks.csv` and is excluded from the median with its count printed; it is never silently dropped.

## 6. Stationary plant (the control for the UNDECIDED gate)

`stationary_track_share` is the gate G359 could not decide, because M0 already cleared it on 0.911765 of
the sealed sections. This row tests the other direction: that a GENUINELY stationary track is still
caught after the collapse. PLANT, sealed, into a COPY of the adapted table (no file under `data/` is
written):

    tracks_unplanted = distinct player `track_id` of the section
    plant_n          = max(3, ceil(0.30 * tracks_unplanted))
    placement        = one row on EVERY distinct player frame, at the fixed position
                       (x_median + i, y_median) for the i-th planted track, i in 0..plant_n-1,
                       with x_median / y_median the medians of the section player x and y; every
                       planted track is EXACTLY frozen, i.e. byte-identical on every frame
    track ids        = max(numeric player track_id) + 1 + i, so no existing id is touched and
                       no original row is modified

Expected planted share `plant_n / (tracks_unplanted + plant_n) >= 0.30/1.30 = 0.230769`, above the
sealed threshold 0.149 by construction. The plant is genuinely stationary under BOTH arms: under M0 its
summed step distance is 0; under M1 it collapses to one surviving row per track, which has no step and
whose displacement the landed metric reindexes to 0.0. SECTIONS: exactly 10 (satisfying the spec's `>=
10`), chosen by the sealed helper `even_pick(selected, 10)` -- the members at indices `round(i * (n - 1)
/ 9)` for `i` in `0..9` over the ordered selected set. It spans the whole set and ALWAYS includes its
last member, so the first 10 can never be returned when `n > 10` (B7); when `n <= 10` every selected
section is used. BARS: detection `>= 0.80` in BOTH arms, detection being the share of planted sections
whose `stationary_track_share` status is REJECT; and A0 rejection `<= 0.05` on the UNPLANTED selected
sections under M1, which is section 5's cell for the same gate. `plants.csv` records, per section per
mode, the unplanted and planted measurement and status, the threshold, `plant_n` and `tracks_unplanted`,
so both bars are recomputable.

## 8. Reach table and eye check

Per gate, per mode, over the selected fresh sections, arm A0: `pass_n`, `reject_n`, `not_applicable_n`,
`other_n` (UNIDENTIFIABLE / REFUSED / MEASURED_NO_BAR) and `share_reachable = (pass_n + reject_n) / n`.
`causes` lists every DISTINCT `reason` string of the rows that did not reach PASS or REJECT, verbatim,
so each blocked gate is named. A NOT_APPLICABLE row is counted in `not_applicable_n`, is excluded from
every share numerator, and NEVER passes a required gate -- asserted by the per-file test. G358 measured
`frame_size_source` absent on every sealed section, blocking the seven containment and attempted-frame
gates there; the same field is recorded per fresh section in `fresh_sections.csv` and its distribution
is reported, not assumed.

EYE CHECK (REQUIRED). 10 sections, chosen by the same sealed `even_pick(selected, 10)` helper as section
6 (never a head slice, B7). Per section ONE track -- the player track with the most rows, ties to the
smallest sorted track id -- rendered as a single SVG strip under `strips/`: the frame axis spans the
section, the upper mark row is that track's M0 rows, the lower mark row its M1 surviving ticks. Each
strip carries the section id, the track id, the frame range and both counts as text, and is asserted `<=
200 KB`; a strip that would exceed it raises rather than being silently thinned. The strips come from
ONE run and are labelled as such (B11).
