## 3. CONVENTIONS, fixed now (identical to the G309 seal)

Footpoint = bbox bottom-centre `((bbox_x1+bbox_x2)/2, bbox_y2)`. A step is two CONSECUTIVE
observations of the SAME `player_id` ordered by `frame`, with NO interpolation across a frame
gap. Displacement is Euclidean image px divided by the CSV's `source_height`. p95 is
NEAREST-RANK, `sorted[ceil(0.95*n)-1]`, no interpolation. An observation with a non-finite
`bbox_x1`, `bbox_x2` or `bbox_y2`, or a non-finite `frame`, is dropped before pairing and
counted. `stride_g` = the MODAL positive difference between consecutive distinct `frame`
values in that game's CSV. `gap` = `frame_b - frame_a` in source frames.

## 4. CANDIDATE DEFINITION AND GAP SPLIT, fixed now

A CANDIDATE is a step whose normalised displacement is **strictly above 0.30** of frame
height. 0.30 comes from the G315 spec and MAY NOT be moved. Per game report the candidate
count, its share of `n_steps`, and the candidate count in each of three gap bins, fixed now:
**B1 `gap == stride_g`** (one stride), **B2 `stride_g < gap <= 5*stride_g`**,
**B3 `gap > 5*stride_g`**. A large step across a large gap is NOT the same claim as a large
step across one stride and the two are reported separately, never pooled into one number.

## 5. EXAMPLE SAMPLING RULE, fixed now (named before any panel exists)

Per game: sort the candidates ASCENDING by normalised step; take the 10 at nearest-rank
decile MIDPOINTS, i.e. ranks `ceil((2i+1)/20 * n_cand)` for i = 0..9, 1-indexed, deduplicated;
if `n_cand < 10` take all candidates. This is an EVEN sweep of the candidate distribution, not
a top-10 slice: a top-10 sheet only ever shows the tail (contract A3/B7).

## 6. RATER RUBRIC, fixed now (before any panel is rendered or seen)

Each panel shows frame A and frame B of ONE candidate step, both boxes drawn, with the
`player_id`, the two frame indices and the normalised step printed on it. Each of two
independent model raters labels every one of the 30 panels with exactly one of:

- **SWAP** -- the box in frame B encloses a visibly DIFFERENT person than the box in frame A
  (different jersey colour, different number, or plainly a different individual), or box B
  encloses no person at all while box A does.
- **NOT-SWAP** -- box B plausibly encloses the SAME person as box A; **OR** a camera cut,
  replay, zoom or graphic transition is visible between the two frames, so the displacement is
  explained by the scene moving rather than by the identity moving.
- **UNREADABLE** -- a box is empty, off-frame, or too small or blurred to identify a person, or
  the panel is otherwise not judgeable.

**PRECEDENCE, fixed now: if a camera cut / replay / zoom transition is visible, the label is
NOT-SWAP regardless of who is in the boxes.** A cut is an innocent cause named in the spec and
this row screens for association errors only.
