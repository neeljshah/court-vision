## Fixed protocol

The 30 real contexts are selected by sorting the 49 retained contexts by
`video_id`, `section_id`, and numeric `frame_order`, then taking
`floor(j*48/29+0.5)` for j=0 through 29. The literal selection CSV bytes are
hashed and all 11 original decode failures remain outside this conditional
native study. Six 640x540 native tiles use their inherited integer offsets.

The control renderer uses seed 388, the inherited 6-pixel white and 10-pixel
black bands, finite length 240, the six tile centres, and a 0/30/60 degree
cycle. It creates 30 opaque controls on those backgrounds and archives pixels
and truth before rating. Raters see pixels only, target an arbitrary visible
fragment, and make three native clicks: the first and second are at least 60
pixels apart and the third is an independently chosen interior point.

Every control needs both raters, all three points at most 3 pixels
perpendicular from the finite known centreline, and projected points within
the band. At least 27 of 30 controls pass before real labels are collected.
Missing clicks are failures. There is no retry, instruction change, or
tolerance change.

After controls pass, two isolated raters mark up to two real fragments per
context in fixed tile order using only the families
SIDELINE, BASELINE, CENTRE_LINE, LANE_LINE, FREE_THROW,
THREE_POINT_STRAIGHT, or UNKNOWN, plus ABSENT and UNKNOWN states. Pair once
by tile and family, then by smallest symmetric line distance with stable ties.
Each third point must be within 3 pixels of its own first-two line, and all
three points on either fragment must be within 6 pixels of the other infinite
line. The audit is pair-specific: without traces Claude records visibility,
then verifies same physical band and at least 8 of 9 evenly spaced points on
visible paint within 3 pixels for each reported fragment. Different parallel
bands, logos, and UNKNOWN do not qualify.

The finisher accounts for all 30 real contexts, family disagreement, and
ABSENT/UNKNOWN. Historical trace generations are rescored only after new
labels are sealed and are marked retrospective. No extractor, filter, fitter,
or homography route runs. The sign convention for any later delta is
improvement = baseline loss minus candidate loss; positive means candidate
better. This preparation has no measured delta.

## Fixed bars and boundaries

The known-band control bar is at least 27 of 30, with both raters and every
point within 3 pixels. A control failure ends the attempt PARTIAL and prevents
real scoring. A complete real diagnostic is DONE only if that control bar
passes; it has no invented minimum recovery bar. Full identity receipts and
all planned states are mandatory. The evaluator, ledger, register, flags,
production routes, and `data/` remain unchanged. Measurement is on the PC
under the RAM gate; no GPU or pod job is authorized by this preparation.
