## Selection rule

No inference output, projection, homography, detector result, or eligibility label was viewed while drawing these positions.

`duration` is the **measured decodable span**, not the container header. `wnba_04` is a partial download: ffprobe reports `partial file`, its last video packet is at 3147.666667 s, 46 s short of the declared 3193.666667 s, and a seek past that point produces no frame at all. It is therefore stratified over 3147.666667 s. `wnba_01` decodes to 5814.300000 s and keeps its full header duration. Neither source's sealed bytes or SHA256 changes; pass A never hit the boundary because its last `wnba_04` midpoint was 3140.438889 s.
