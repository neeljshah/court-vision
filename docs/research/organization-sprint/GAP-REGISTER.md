# GAP-REGISTER -- organization-sprint audit

Deduplicated, severity-sorted register of every gap found across 46 structured audits of the
calibrated 4-sport prediction sprint (eval-gate keystone, funnel levers, build-loop scaffolding,
sellable calibration layer, offline RAG, PROPOSED-* human-confirm docs).

## Honest summary

The sprint's **honesty/leak DISCIPLINE is genuinely intact** where it is the design center: no
fabricated `$`-ROI/+EV/picks copy ships live, retracted numbers appear only inside guard tests and
retraction framing, the LLM authors no scored number, edge_claimed=False is pervasive, and BSS<=0 /
ABSTAIN / market-efficient are correctly first-class successes. The eval-gate's core math (BSS, ECE,
Murphy decomposition, cluster-robust DM, Shin devig) is provably correct.

The defects cluster into **five themes**: (1) **string-compare vintage guards** that silently
mis-order date-vs-datetime, producing both a real same-day-leak class and a same-day over-drop that
destroys the freshness workflow; (2) a **predict->ledger->track-record chain that does not actually
work end-to-end** (key mismatch yields zero rows for tennis/NBA, missing game_date disables the leak
guard, concurrent append loses rows); (3) **pervasive provenance/honesty drift** -- the NBA in-game
"0.209->0.159 WIN" headline is real-corpus-only and is CONTRADICTED by the committed fixtures
(no-improvement), the calibration banner shows NBA numbers for every sport and labels a synthetic
anchor "last OOS Brier", and scoreboards print fixture numbers under "SAME real outcomes"; (4) a
**bare-module-name test-collision + per-file-pytest-from-root failure** that is no longer
hypothetical (reproduced across eval_gate/ledger/freshness, duplicate `schema.py`/`ledger.py`/
`test_ledger.py`); and (5) **funnel pieces not wired to the judge they're sold under** -- the shipped
in-game repricer, freshness lever, and ledger are each covered only by isolated tests, never the
eval-gate's block decision, while the RAG/MCP and product CLI are partly uncommitted/untested.

**Counts after dedup: 9 P0, 34 P1, 35 P2 (78 total).** Overall coherence verdict: the *components*
are individually sound and honestly framed, but the *seams* (vintage compares, predict->ledger,
doc-vs-fixture numbers, test plumbing, gate scope) are where the system is weakest and where a
skeptical buyer or a single `git add -A` / multi-file pytest run would expose real problems.
Several P0/P1 are latent today (dodged by the committed synthetic golden config) but must be fixed
before the real-corpus human-run path or the sellable board ships.
