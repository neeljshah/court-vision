## STREAM 1 — Personal Betting

### The Strategy
- Retail books only (your own capital)
- Extract maximum edge while accounts are open
- Half Kelly always — ruin probability near zero
- Compound 100% — never pull profits mid-season
- Only bet when edge > 6% (vig eats you below that)

### The 10 Books
| Book | Why |
|------|-----|
| DraftKings | Deepest prop menu |
| FanDuel | Softest same-game parlays |
| BetMGM | Slow to limit winners |
| Caesars | Promos add free EV |
| ESPN Bet | New — still soft lines |
| Fanatics | Very new, very soft |
| BetRivers | Regional, less sharp money |
| Hard Rock | Soft on props |
| PrizePicks | No vig, never limits accounts |
| Underdog Fantasy | Same — no vig, pure edge |

### Edge By Bet Type
| Market | Your Edge Ceiling |
|--------|-----------------|
| Game spreads/totals | 1-3% |
| Player props | 4-8% |
| Live betting (CV spatial moat) | 8-15% |
| Obscure props (first basket, combos) | 5-12% |
| Blended average | 5-8% |

### Progression
```
$10K start  →  Half Kelly, $200-400/bet, 6%+ edge only
Books limit you after 2-4 weeks of winning (expected)
Shift limited accounts to minimum (keep for promos)
Add offshore: Pinnacle, Bookmaker, BetOnline
Personal ceiling solo: $1-5M/year
```

### Pinnacle Test
If you beat Pinnacle's closing line consistently — you have world-class edge. They are the sharpest book alive. Beating them by 1%+ is elite.

## STREAM 2 — Fund Management (OPM)

### The Structure
```
Investors → LLC → Exchange accounts (LLC name)
                      ↓
              Every bet visible, auditable
                      ↓
              Quarterly distributions
```

### Why Exchanges Only For OPM
- Zero ToS issues (no third-party betting restrictions)
- No limits ever — scales with AUM
- Full audit trail — investors can verify every bet
- No account risk — cleaner investor pitch
- Lower edge than retail (3-6%) but 10x the sizing → better absolute returns

### The Three Exchanges
| Exchange | Best For | Commission |
|----------|----------|------------|
| Novig | All markets, most US states | ~1.5% on winnings |
| Sporttrade | NFL, NBA | ~2% on winnings |
| Betfair (international) | Everything — deepest liquidity | 2-5% on winnings |

### Fee Structure
- **Start:** 0% management / 25-30% performance (unproven — only charge on profits)
- **Year 2+:** 1% management / 20% performance (standard after track record)
- **Established:** 2% management / 20% performance (hedge fund standard)

### AUM Growth Path
| Year | AUM | Annual Fee Income |
|------|-----|------------------|
| 1 | $100K-500K | $15-40K |
| 2 | $1M-5M | $150-500K |
| 3 | $5M-20M | $600K-3M |
| 4 | $20M-100M | $3M-15M |
| 5+ | $100M-500M | $15M-50M |

### Legal Path
| Capital Raised | Structure Needed | Cost |
|---------------|-----------------|------|
| Under $1M | Simple LLC + operating agreement | $500-2K |
| $1M-5M | Reg D 506(b) filing | $20-40K legal |
| $5M-50M | RIA registration with SEC | $50-100K |
| $50M+ | Full fund + compliance officer | $150K+ |

**Rule:** Never promise returns. Show audited track record. "Past performance does not guarantee future results" on every document.

## STREAM 5 — Prediction Markets

### Why This Is The Biggest Near-Term Unlock
Kalshi and Polymarket are **right now** the most inefficient markets that accept intelligent capital. Almost no quants are in them yet. Your models translate directly.

| Platform | Type | Key Advantage |
|----------|------|---------------|
| Kalshi | CFTC regulated, real money | Legally cannot limit accounts |
| Polymarket | Crypto-based, global | Massive liquidity, no restrictions |
| Sporttrade | US exchange | No limits, auditable |

### Examples of Contracts Your Models Hit
- "Will LeBron score 25+ tonight?" → your props model
- "Will the Warriors cover -4.5?" → your game model
- "Will there be overtime?" → your OT probability model (M15)

### Why The Edge Is Higher Here
Books have decades of experience pricing lines. Kalshi launched in 2021. The market is in its infancy. **First-mover advantage is massive right now.**

Estimated edge: **8-12%** on player performance contracts (vs 4-8% on sportsbooks)
